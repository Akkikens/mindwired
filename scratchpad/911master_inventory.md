### ⚡ CONFLICTS — same event, competing accounts, called out loudly

**1. WHO GAVE THE NATIONAL LANDING/GROUND ORDER, AND WHEN — 9:42 vs 9:45.**
This is the single most significant cross-file conflict, and it is already
flagged inside `CLAIMS-thegrounding.md` itself as "own this, don't flatten it"
(Act 4). Two men, two accounts:
- The **9/11 Commission Report, verbatim**: at 9:42, FAA Command Center
  national operations manager **Ben Sliney** — on his first day in the job —
  "ordered all FAA facilities to instruct all aircraft to land at the nearest
  airport." The Commission's Chapter 1 **does not mention Mineta in connection
  with this order at all.**
- **Norman Mineta's own sworn Commission testimony** (23 May 2003): "at
  approximately 9:45 a.m. ... I gave the FAA the final order for all civil
  aircraft to land at the nearest airport as soon as possible."
- **The FAA's own institutional timeline (2026, current)**: "9:45 AM: At the
  direction of Transportation Secretary Norman Mineta, the FAA Command Center
  closed all U.S. airspace for the first time in history."
- A third, unverified account (**ALLEGED, USA Today 2002**) has Mineta on the
  phone to Belger: "F— pilot discretion. Monte, bring down all the planes" —
  minutes after Sliney's order.
- An UNVERIFIED gloss (Newsday 2007) claims the Commission found Mineta gave
  the order "about an hour after Sliney did" — GRD explicitly flags that this
  sentence **appears nowhere in the Commission's actual text** and must not be
  repeated.
- GRD's own resolution: "two men gave a landing order within three minutes of
  each other. The Commission credits the operational one to the man on the
  floor; the Secretary's is the ratification from the bunker" — and explicitly
  bars ever saying Mineta "took credit falsely."

**2. NORAD's FALSE 9:16/9:24 NOTIFICATION TIMES — documented independently
in two separate fact-packs, not contradictory but a major duplication to
reconcile before scripting.** Both `CLAIMS-noradtapes.md` and
`CLAIMS-flight93.md` independently build an entire act around the identical
underlying fact: NORAD's May 2003 testimony claimed NEADS got hijack notice of
UA93 at 9:16 and of AA77 at 9:24 — both false. The 9:16 entry actually recorded
UA175's tail number (N612UA); the 9:24 entry recorded AA11's tail number
(N334AA). Both files cite the same Col. Marr e-mail ("it is easiest to explain
the simultaneous scramble order with the AA77 notification as the scramble
being against AA77...") as the single best on-screen exhibit. **No factual
contradiction between the two files — but a master timeline must not use both
files' full treatments back-to-back or the episode repeats itself.**

**3. WHEN DID NEW YORK CENTER DECLARE "ATC ZERO" — 9:03 vs 9:05.** Flagged
inside `CLAIMS-thegrounding.md` itself: "The Commission and SS17 say 9:05;
Michael McCormick's own retelling says 9:03. **Use 9:05.**" A same-file, not
cross-file, discrepancy, but it belongs in the master conflict list because it
touches the same minute-window as the UA175 impact (9:03:11) and could easily

**4. HOW MANY CONTROLLERS RECORDED THE DESTROYED "BAT CAVE" TAPE — five vs
six.** `CLAIMS-noradtapes.md`: the DOT OIG's own report says **five** air
traffic controllers made the destroyed audiotape statement; contemporaneous

**5. UA93's CRASH TIME — 10:03:11 vs a seismic-study 10:06.** Both `F93` and
