# DIMENSION FACT PACK — American 11, United 175, and the Tower Evacuations
### (Black Box Breakdown, `sept11timeline`)

**Scope:** the two hijacked-aircraft impacts on the World Trade Center as human
events (not engineering data — that's already covered in
`docs/planning/CLAIMS-wtccollapse.md`), and the evacuation of WTC 1 and WTC 2,
which no existing fact-pack in this repo covers.

**Labels (same convention as every other CLAIMS/dimension file in this repo):**
- **CONFIRMED** — sourced, safe to state as fact.
- **DISPUTED** — credible sources disagree; state both, attributed.
- **ALLEGED (by whom)** — attributed to a named party, not independently settled.
- **UNVERIFIED** — repeated widely but not confirmed against a primary source.
- **EXCLUDE** — do not use under any circumstance.

🧍 marks currently-living named people (family members, survivors, officials
who are still alive as of 2026). No claim below assigns thoughts, feelings,
or final-moment interiority to anyone beyond what the cited record states.

## 4. THE EVACUATION — the core of this dimension

**Primary source for every number below: NIST NCSTAR 1-7 ("Occupant
Behavior, Egress, and Emergency Communications"), corroborated by 9/11
Commission Report Ch. 9 ("Heroism and Horror") narratively.**

### Occupancy at time of first impact
- NIST: **"NIST estimates that there were 8,900 ± 750 people in WTC 1 at
  8:46:30 a.m."** — CONFIRMED, NCSTAR 1-7.
- NIST: **"NIST estimates that there were 8,540 ± 920 people inside WTC 2 at
  8:46:30 a.m."** — CONFIRMED, NCSTAR 1-7.
- Combined: **"17,400 ± 1,180 occupants inside WTC 1 and WTC 2 at 8:46:30
  a.m."** — CONFIRMED, NCSTAR 1-7. (Note: this 8:46:30 timestamp is
  NCSTAR 1-7's own internal impact-time figure, ten seconds off the
  8:46:40 used elsewhere — see CONFLICT note above; it does not change the
  occupancy estimate.)
- 9/11 Commission Report's independent, more casual figure for a typical
  workday, used for scale-setting narration rather than the precise 9/11
  headcount: **"up to 50,000 office workers occupied the towers, and 40,000
  people passed through the complex"** on an ordinary day. CONFIRMED, Ch. 9.
  (This is a different, larger figure than NIST's 17,400 because it
  describes total daily building traffic, not people present at 8:46 a.m.
  — do not conflate the two numbers on screen.)

### How many got out
- Of the ~17,400 NIST-estimated occupants, NIST's own casualty estimate
  across the two towers is **2,146 to 2,163 deaths** — CONFIRMED, NCSTAR
  1-7. Arithmetically that leaves roughly 15,200–15,300 who survived (NIST
  does not itself headline a rounded "X% evacuated safely" figure in the
  excerpt retrieved this session — the survivor count is a derived
  subtraction, not a NIST-stated percentage, so present it as "roughly
  15,000 people got out" rather than quoting a NIST percentage that wasn't
  found verbatim).
- **UNVERIFIED as a NIST-stated figure:** any rounded "70–80% evacuated"
  or "99% of people below the impact floors survived" framing should be
  treated as UNVERIFIED unless the specific NIST sentence stating that
  percentage is located and quoted directly — do not present a derived
  percentage as if NIST said it verbatim.

### Stairwells — why Stairwell A in the South Tower mattered
- WTC 1 (North Tower) had **three central stairwells** (A, B, C) running
  "essentially from top to bottom," plus 99 elevators. Stairwells A and C
  ran from the 110th floor to the mezzanine; Stairwell B ran from the 107th
  floor down to six floors below ground (B6). CONFIRMED, 9/11 Commission
  Ch. 9.
- AA11's impact (floors 93–99) made **"all three of the building's
  stairwells... impassable from the 92nd floor up"** in WTC 1. CONFIRMED,
  Ch. 9. This is the structural reason no one evacuated from above the
  impact zone in the North Tower.
- In WTC 2 (South Tower), after UA175's impact, **"one of the stairwells
  (A) initially remained passable from at least the 91st floor down, and
  likely from top to bottom."** CONFIRMED, Ch. 9. This is the single most
  important evacuation fact in this dimension: Stairwell A survived the
  impact largely intact because of where in the building's core it sat
  relative to the aircraft's path, and it was the one route by which people
  from above the South Tower's impact floors could descend.
- NIST NCSTAR 1-7, independently: **"at least 18 individuals used Stairwell
  A, located in the northwest corner and furthest from the impact damage, to
  descend below the 78th floor to evacuate the building."** CONFIRMED,
  NCSTAR 1-7. (NIST's own occupant-population impact-zone band for WTC 2 is
  given as floors 78–84 in this specific section, versus the 77–85
  structural-damage band from NCSTAR 1 — both are legitimate NIST figures
  describing overlapping but not identical things; do not present them as
  contradictory on screen, just don't merge them into one number.)
- By 9:59 a.m. (South Tower collapse), Commission Report: **"at least one
  person had descended from as high as the 91st floor of that tower, and
  stairwell A was reported to have been almost empty."** CONFIRMED, Ch. 9.

### Elevators
- NIST NCSTAR 1-7: **"Sixteen percent of the survivors used elevators to
  evacuate"** (WTC 2). CONFIRMED.
- NCSTAR 1-7 separately documents **"Fatalities in Elevators"** as a
  distinct finding category — some occupants died trapped in elevator cars.
  CONFIRMED that this category exists in the report; a specific death count
  for this category was not located and quoted in this session's research —
  **UNVERIFIED pending a direct quote of the number** if the script needs
  one; do not guess a figure.

### The 9:59 and 10:28 collapses, relative to who was still inside
- **9:58:59 a.m.** — South Tower (WTC 2) collapse-initiation, in
  approximately 10 seconds, described by the Commission Report as **"killing
  all civilians and emergency personnel inside."** CONFIRMED, Ch. 9. (Cross-
  reference: `CLAIMS-wtccollapse.md`/master inventory round this to "9:59.")
- **10:28:25 a.m.** — North Tower (WTC 1) collapse, described by the
  Commission Report as **"killing all civilians alive on upper floors,"**
  with a specific documented exception: **"twelve firefighters, one PAPD
  officer, and three civilians who were descending stairwell B of the North
  Tower survived its collapse."** CONFIRMED, Ch. 9. This is a precise,
  citable survivor detail — safe to use on screen exactly as stated,
  without embellishment about how or why they survived beyond "they were
  descending stairwell B."

## 5. SURVIVABILITY — NIST's above/below-impact-zone finding

This is the single clearest, most citable NIST number set for this
dimension and should likely anchor the survivability beat of the script.

- **WTC 1 (North Tower), impact floors 93–99:** NIST NCSTAR 1-7 states
  **"No occupant evacuated from above the 91st floor."** CONFIRMED. Total
  WTC 1 deaths are given as approximately **1,500**, of which **107** are
  attributed to occupants who were **below** the 92nd floor (i.e., below
  the impassable-stairwell line) at the time of collapse. CONFIRMED,
  NCSTAR 1-7. That leaves the overwhelming majority of WTC 1's deaths —
  roughly 1,393 by direct subtraction — among people above the 91st/92nd
  floor line, for whom every stairwell was severed by the impact itself
  and no evacuation was structurally possible. Present the 107-below /
  ~1,393-above split as NIST's own finding; present "~1,393" explicitly as
  a subtraction from NIST's two stated numbers (1,500 total, 107 below),
  not as a number NIST itself wrote — label it **CONFIRMED (derived from
  two NIST-stated figures)**, not "CONFIRMED, NIST states 1,393."
- **WTC 2 (South Tower), impact floors 78–84 (NCSTAR 1-7's band):** NIST
  states **"only 11 occupants initially below the 78th floor were killed
  when WTC 2 collapsed,"** against approximately **630** total WTC 2
  deaths. CONFIRMED, NCSTAR 1-7. By the same subtraction logic, roughly
  619 WTC 2 deaths were among occupants at or above the impact zone —
  label this the same way, **CONFIRMED (derived)**.
- The qualitative NIST point to carry into the script: South Tower
  occupants below the impact zone had a far higher survival rate than
  North Tower occupants below its impact zone, because Stairwell A
  remained usable in the South Tower and the South Tower's occupants also
  had roughly 16 minutes' additional warning (from AA11's impact on the
  North Tower) before UA175 struck. This causal link (warning time +
  Stairwell A survival → dramatically better below-impact-zone outcomes in
  WTC 2 vs WTC 1) is a reasonable synthesis of the two CONFIRMED numbers
  above and Ch. 9's stairwell findings — present it as analysis, not as a
  single NIST sentence, since no single quoted NIST sentence stating this
  causal comparison directly was located this session.
- NIST's own framing of the evacuation-success problem: **"Success in
  evacuating a building in an emergency can be characterized by two
  quantities: the time people needed to evacuate and the time available for
  them to do so."** CONFIRMED, NCSTAR 1-7.
- NIST modeling finding on the razor-thin margin: the collapse times **"were
  considerably shorter, by a factor of three or four, than the time needed
  to clear the tenant spaces of WTC 1 following the 1993 bombing and an
  additional factor of two shorter than the time needed to clear the last
  person from the elevators in the building."** CONFIRMED, NCSTAR 1-7. NIST
  further modeled a counterfactual: **"approximately 14,000 occupants would
  have been unable to evacuate from WTC 1 and WTC 2 on September 11, 2001,
  had the starting building population been 19,800 in each building"** (i.e.
  near full daytime occupancy rather than the actual lower 8:46 a.m.
  population). CONFIRMED, NCSTAR 1-7 — this is NIST's own explicit
  statement that the actual death toll would have been catastrophically
  higher had the attack occurred at a time of fuller building occupancy
  (e.g., mid-morning rather than 8:46 a.m., or on a day without the routine
  early-morning below-capacity population). Use this NIST counterfactual
  carefully and only as NIST's own stated modeling conclusion — do not
  extend it into further speculation about "what if" scenarios beyond what
  NIST wrote.

## 6. THE 1993 BOMBING'S EFFECT ON THE 2001 EVACUATION

- NIST NCSTAR 1-7 explicitly compares the two events and addresses this
  question directly (see the collapse-time-vs-1993-clearance-time finding
  quoted in Section 5 above).
- **Prior direct experience:** **"Sixteen percent of 2001 WTC evacuees were
  also present during the 1993 bombing, although many other occupants were
  also knowledgeable about the 1993 evacuation."** CONFIRMED, NCSTAR 1-7.
- **Drill participation:** **"Two-thirds of the occupants had participated
  in at least one fire drill during the 12 months immediately prior to
  September 11, 2001."** CONFIRMED, NCSTAR 1-7. (This reflects post-1993
  evacuation-drill policy changes at the WTC, though the report's own
  causal attribution of the drill requirement specifically to 1993 was not
  independently quoted this session — reasonable to state as background
  policy context, not as a direct NIST causal claim, unless the specific
  sentence is located.)
- **Physical/way-finding aids:** **"The most commonly mentioned forms of
  aid were assistance from coworkers and emergency responders and the
  photoluminescent markings in stairwells."** CONFIRMED, NCSTAR 1-7 — this
  confirms photoluminescent (glow-in-the-dark) stairwell markings were
  present and used by evacuees in 2001.
- **UNVERIFIED — the specific mechanism/date linking photoluminescent
  markings and stairwell lighting improvements directly to a post-1993
  building-code or Port Authority retrofit decision.** NCSTAR 1-7 has a
  section titled "Changes to the Egress System after the 1993 Bombing"
  (its own Section 2.4), but this session's research tooling could not
  retrieve that section's specific text (the archive.org OCR fetch cut off
  before reaching it). **Do not state specific claims about what changed
  after 1993 (e.g., "battery backup lighting was added," "stairwell doors
  were repainted with glow strips in year X") without pulling the actual
  text of NCSTAR 1-7 Section 2.4** — flag this as a research gap for
  scripting, not something to fill with plausible-sounding detail.
