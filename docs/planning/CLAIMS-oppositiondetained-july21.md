## LAW (neutral, sourced)
- **Detention vs arrest:** detention at a protest = short-term preventive holding (usually released same day, no charge); arrest = custody for an alleged offence with procedural safeguards.
- **BNSS 2023:** Sec 163 (= CrPC 144, prohibitory orders); Sec 170 (= CrPC 151, preventive arrest); **Sec 172 (NEW — detain/remove a person disregarding a lawful police direction, release within 24h)** = the "preventive removal" power at a protest. PM residence = high-security/VVIP zone, not a designated protest site.
- **Article 19(1)(b)** right to assemble peaceably + **19(3)** reasonable restrictions (public order); courts: "regulation cannot become prohibition."
