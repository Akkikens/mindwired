## A. WHAT HAPPENED — CONFIRMED

- **Wednesday 26 August 2026**, water rising ~**09:00 local**.
- Origin: **Lhende Khola** (Lende), a transboundary stream rising in **Gyirong
  (Kyirong/Jilong) County, Tibet**, flowing south into Nepal. Source coords
  **28.2765 N, 85.5194 E** (Petley 🧍).
- Chain: Lhende Khola → **Bhotekoshi** → **Trishuli** → **Narayani** (= **Gandak**
  in India). District **Rasuwa**, then Nuwakot, Dhading, Chitwan, Gorkha, Tanahun,
  Nawalparasi E & W.
- **Mechanism (best current reconstruction):** a ~**600 m wide** section of
  **glacier plus underlying bedrock** detached at ~**5,200 m** and fell ~**1,200
  m** into the Lhende valley. The mass **blocked the Lhende Khola**; water
  impounded behind it; **the temporary dam burst**.
- Volume **100-200 million m³** — **[ALLEGED — Mohd. Farooq Azam 🧍, ICIMOD;
  single source, early estimate]**.
- Nepal's DHM (Binod Parajuli 🧍): **ice avalanche + permafrost movement +
  rockslide**, and the avalanche **originated on Nepali territory** then crossed
  into China.
- Flood wave travelled **~100 km**. Trishuli rose **9 m in 30 minutes at
  Galchchi**, 7 m at Malekhu. Several DHM gauging stations destroyed.
- **Göran Ekström 🧍** (Lamont-Doherty): flood front **40-50 m high** at ~**70
  km/h**. Eyewitness Rajendra Dhungana 🧍 (Rasuwa chief customs officer): a wall of
  water ~100 m high; *"Timure bazar was wiped out in a jiffy."*
