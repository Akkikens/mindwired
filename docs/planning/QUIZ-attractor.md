# The Great Attractor — Quiz (7 questions)

**1. How fast is the Milky Way being dragged through space toward the Great Attractor?**
A) 600 km/h  B) **~600 km/s — about 2 million km/h ✓**  C) The speed of light  D) It isn't moving
→ Measured against the oldest light in the universe.

**2. How did we first realize our galaxy is moving at all?**
A) Watching nearby stars  B) **A slight warm/cool imbalance in the cosmic microwave background ✓**  C) GPS satellites  D) Hubble photos
→ The CMB "dipole" — one half of the sky is very slightly warmer.

**3. Why couldn't we see the Great Attractor directly for decades?**
A) It's invisible  B) It hides behind the Sun  C) **It sits behind our own galaxy's dust — the Zone of Avoidance ✓**  D) It's too small
→ Roughly 1/5 of the entire sky is blocked by the Milky Way's disk.

**4. Roughly how massive is the Great Attractor?**
A) About 1 Milky Way  B) ~100 Milky Ways  C) **Tens of thousands of Milky Ways ✓**  D) Heavier than the whole universe
→ A colossal concentration centered near the Norma Cluster.

**5. What is "Laniakea"?**
A) A black hole  B) **Our galaxy supercluster — ~100,000 galaxies; Hawaiian for "immense heaven" ✓**  C) A NASA mission  D) A kind of star
→ Defined in 2014 by mapping how galaxies flow; the Great Attractor is the bottom of its basin.

**6. Besides the Attractor *pulling* us, what else moves our galaxy?**
A) Nothing  B) **A giant empty void *behind* us, pushing — the Dipole Repeller ✓**  C) The Moon  D) A dark-matter wind
→ Discovered in 2017: emptiness has less gravity, so it effectively pushes.

**7. Will we ever actually reach the Great Attractor?**
A) Yes, in a billion years  B) Yes, we're nearly there  C) **Almost certainly never — dark energy's expansion wins ✓**  D) We'll collide with it
→ Laniakea isn't gravitationally bound; the finish line races away faster than we cross it.
