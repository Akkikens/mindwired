## 9. LAW — the Article 19 close (neutral, textbook)

- **Art. 19(1)(a)** freedom of speech & expression; **19(1)(b)** right *"to assemble peaceably and without arms."*
- **Reasonable restrictions:** 19(2) (public order, security, etc.) and **19(3)** (public order; sovereignty/integrity) — restrictions must be *reasonable*.
- **BNSS §163** (successor to CrPC §144): magistrate may bar assemblies to prevent danger/disturbance; order lapses in 2 months (extendable to 6).
- **Case law (one-line neutral holdings):** *In re Ramlila Maidan* (2012) — peaceful assembly protected, police action must be proportionate; *Mazdoor Kisan Shakti Sangathan v. UoI* (2018) — protest can be regulated **but not banned** (the Jantar Mantar case); *Anita Thakur v. State of J&K* (2016) — police may use **reasonable** force on an unlawful assembly, but **excessive force is impermissible**; *Anuradha Bhasin* (2020) — internet-suspension orders must be published, proportionate, reviewable.
