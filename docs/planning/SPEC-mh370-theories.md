## HONESTY & DEFAMATION PLAN (read before scripting — stricter than usual)
1. **Every theory gets the same frame:** the claim → the case FOR → the case AGAINST →
   a verdict (plausible / unlikely / debunked / unprovable). No theory is asserted as fact.
2. **Captain Zaharie (deliberate-diversion theory):** real, deceased, living family.
   Present as the theory investigators lean toward but NEVER proven; simulator route =
   circumstantial coincidence the official report could not conclusively link; official
   Malaysian 2018 verdict ("cause undetermined; third-party interference not ruled out")
   gets the last word. Never state guilt.
3. **State-level conspiracies (shot-down/cover-up, Diego Garcia, the "Northwoods /
   MH17-swap" claim from the comments):** name them as theories that EXIST, explain WHY the
   physical evidence (Inmarsat southern arc + debris drifting onto African beaches)
   contradicts them, and move on. Do NOT endorse or lend credibility to an unfounded
   accusation against any government or named party. Debunk with evidence, don't amplify.
4. **"Orbs / teleportation / aliens":** the viral "orb" videos are confirmed CGI hoaxes —
   say so plainly and kindly. Debunk, don't mock the audience.
5. **No fabricated cockpit dialogue / no CVR** (none was recovered). ATC recreations only,
   labeled "ATC — RECREATION". No image/clip claimed as the real event.
6. Passengers/families with dignity.

## Structure (teaser cold open → theory chapters → verdict → the newest lead → close)
COLD OPEN (~30s): "A Boeing 777 with 239 people cannot simply vanish. So what happened to
it? Everyone has a theory. Tonight we test every serious one — and the wild ones — against
the one thing that doesn't lie: the evidence." → wordmark → title.

RE-CAP (short, ~60s): the load-bearing facts every theory must explain — the deliberate
turn-back, transponder off at the border blind spot, the 02:25 SATCOM reboot, 7 Inmarsat
arcs → southern Indian Ocean, 6+ hour flight, fuel-exhaustion steep descent, flaperon +
debris (flaps NOT set for a ditch). These are the "rules of the game" a theory must fit.

THE THEORIES (each = short chapter, FOR / AGAINST / verdict):
1. **Accident — decompression & the "ghost flight."** Incl. the crew **oxygen-cylinder
   rupture** scenario a viewer raised: an O2 bottle bursts, rapid decompression, crew
   incapacitated, autopilot flies on to fuel exhaustion. FOR: explains silence + long
   straight flight + no ditch. AGAINST: doesn't explain the deliberate multi-waypoint
   turn-back or the SATCOM reboot. Verdict: plausible for the END, weak for the START.
2. **Accident — fire / electrical (the "Langkawi turn").** Pilot Chris Goodfellow's theory:
   a fire (e.g. tyre/electrical) forces a turn toward the nearest long runway (Langkawi),
   crew overcome, ghost flight. FOR: the first turn does aim at a real diversion airport.
   AGAINST: debris showed no fire; a fire rarely allows 6 more hours of controlled,
   waypoint-following flight; systems went dark selectively, not like a spreading fire.
   Verdict: unlikely.
3. **Lithium-battery fire in the hold.** FOR: ~200 kg of lithium-ion batteries aboard.
   AGAINST: same as above + investigators found the batteries met packing rules; no fire
   signature on recovered debris. Verdict: unlikely.
4. **Hijacking (third party).** FOR: the flying was deliberate; two stolen passports early
   scare. AGAINST: passports were asylum-seekers (not terror); no claim, no demand, no
   trace; the flight ended in the remotest ocean, not at a destination. Verdict: unlikely.
5. **Deliberate diversion (the leading theory).** The turn flown by someone who knew the
   jet; systems disabled in sequence; SATCOM reboot; steered to the void; the home-sim
   route. FOR (strong, uncomfortable). AGAINST/CAVEAT (never proven; sim inconclusive;
   report undetermined). Verdict: the evidence leans here — but it is NOT proof. Handle per
   defamation rules.
6. **Shot down + cover-up.** FOR: "no fighters were scrambled" (the top comment); militaries
   were watching. AGAINST: a shoot-down over the Strait leaves debris there immediately;
   Inmarsat shows 6 more hours of flight; debris drifted from the SOUTHERN ocean years later.
   Verdict: contradicted by the evidence.
7. **Landed at Diego Garcia (US base).** FOR: a real remote base in the Indian Ocean.
   AGAINST: the arc/Doppler put it thousands of km SOUTH of Diego Garcia; a landed intact
   jet can't also shed a barnacled flaperon onto Réunion. Verdict: debunked.
8. **The wild file — Northwoods/MH17 swap, "orbs"/teleport, alien abduction, staged
   vanish.** Named briefly, then dismissed: the "orb" clips are proven CGI; the geopolitical
   swap and supernatural claims all founder on the same rock — real, confirmed debris from
   9M-MRO washing ashore, and the independent satellite math. Verdict: debunked.

VERDICT CHAPTER: line the theories up against the "rules." What survives: a controlled
diversion (human or a very specific incapacitation) into the southern Indian Ocean, ending
in a high-speed impact. What the evidence CAN'T yet do: prove who, or why. Official position
last.

THE NEWEST LEAD (current, 2026): the **hydrophone/acoustic** re-analysis — a faint
low-frequency signal at Cape Leeuwin from the direction of the 7th arc, being re-examined in
2026; caveat it's a single faint candidate, a lead not a location. + Ocean Infinity search
extended to 2027.

CLOSE: the honest unknown; the families; verbal bridge → the AF447 or Colgan episode;
subscribe.
