## The deliberate-impact history (real footage secured for all three)
- **Ranger 7, 8, 9 (1964–65):** NASA/JPL probes deliberately crashed into the
  Moon, transmitting real television video up to the moment of impact —
  actual footage exists and is fetched
  (`public/shorts/spacexlunarimpact/video/ranger{7,8,9}_impact.m4v`).
  Ranger 9's clip visually confirmed: the real crescent-Moon approach shot,
  then the rapid-zoom multi-camera mosaic seconds before impact. This is
  the episode's strongest real cold-open material — genuine footage of the
  exact event class the episode is about, not a stand-in.
- **Apollo S-IVB booster impacts:** third-stage boosters from several Apollo
  missions were deliberately crashed into the Moon to calibrate seismometers
  left by earlier landings. **No camera ever filmed the moment of impact for
  any of them** — there was no camera on the booster and none on the Moon
  aimed at the site. NASA's SVS "Apollo 13 Booster Impact" piece
  (svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4803) is a **data reconstruction** built from real
  seismometer readings, not raw footage. **If used, it must be labeled on
  screen as a visualization** — same rule as NIST's collapse simulations on
  Black Box. Do not let it play as if it were camera footage.
- **LCROSS (2009):** NASA deliberately crashed a spent Centaur upper stage
  into Cabeus crater to search for water ice (later confirmed present).
  Real NASA TV broadcast footage of the impact is secured
  (`lcross_impact.mp4`).
