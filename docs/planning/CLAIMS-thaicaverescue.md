## ⚠ DATA CORRECTIONS (apply before scripting)

1. **Duangphet "Dom" Phromthep died by suicide — NOT a car crash.** No outlet
   anywhere reports a car crash; that appears to be a misremembering. Coroner
   Prof. Catherine Mason (Leicester Coroner's Court, inquest concluded Oct 4,
   2023) ruled suicide, explicitly stating the reason is unknown and
   unforeseeable. Never state a cause not in the coroner's own words; never
   imply the 2018 cave trauma caused it — no source draws that line.
2. **Musk v. Unsworth was a CIVIL case — "not liable," never "not guilty" or
   "cleared."** The jury (Dec 6, 2019, LA federal court) found Musk not
   liable for defamation, deliberating under an hour. Per juror Carl
   Shusterman's own account, the verdict turned on a narrow technical
   element (whether the tweet could be shown to refer to Unsworth
   specifically, since he wasn't named) — the jury never reached the
   question of whether the underlying accusation was true or false. Do not
   say the jury "ruled the accusation was false" or that Musk was
   "vindicated" — neither is what was decided.
3. **Two rescue-personnel deaths, not one** — Saman Kunan (died July 6, 2018,
   during the operation) and Petty Officer **Beirut Pakbara** (correct
   spelling; died Dec 28, 2019, from a blood infection contracted during his
   rescue diving, 18 months later). Many summaries mention only Kunan.
4. **Sedation protocol is precisely documented, not "press reports vary"**:
   alprazolam 0.5 mg orally, atropine 20 μg/kg IM, ketamine 5 mg/kg IM
   loading dose — per the rescue medical team's own NEJM letter (see §5).
5. Rear Admiral's name: use **"Apakorn Yookongkaew"** (closest to the
   plurality of transliterations found; flag on-screen text for a final
   spelling check regardless — no single spelling was universal).
6. Lt. Col. **Pak Loharachun** (Army 3rd Medical Battalion, SEAL-trained,
   stayed with the group from discovery to the last extraction day) is
   well-sourced — script as CONFIRMED, not a hedge.

## 1. WHO'S WHO

- **The team:** "Wild Boars" (Moo Pa) youth football club, Mae Sai district,
  Chiang Rai. 12 players, ages 11–16, plus assistant coach. CONFIRMED.
- **Ekkapol Chantawong** ("Coach Ek"), 25 at the time — orphaned ~age 10,
  spent years as a novice Buddhist monk, later the team's assistant coach.
  Legally **stateless** at the time of the rescue (Tai Lue ethnic minority),
  as were at least three of the boys. Credited with using meditation to
  keep the group calm and giving up his own food. CONFIRMED throughout.
- **Nopparat Kanthawong** — the team's separate HEAD coach (not in the
  cave), raised the alarm after ~20 missed calls from parents. CONFIRMED —
  do not conflate with Ekkapol.
- **John Volanthen** (IT consultant, Bristol) and **Rick Stanton** (retired
  firefighter, Coventry) — British Cave Rescue Council divers who found the
  group July 2, 2018. Real exchange (helmet-cam, widely circulated):
  "How many of you?" / "Thirteen?" / "Thirteen! Brilliant." CONFIRMED.
- **Jason Mallinson, Chris Jewell** — additional British extraction divers.
  **Dr. Richard "Harry" Harris** (Australian anaesthetist) led the sedation
  plan; **Craig Challen** (Australian vet) his dive partner. **Jim Warny**
  (Belgian-born, Ireland-based) extracted Ekkapol on the final day.
  CONFIRMED.
- **Saman Kunan**, 37, former Thai Navy SEAL volunteer — died July 6, 2018,
  asphyxiated returning from delivering air tanks to Chamber 3; posthumously
  promoted seven ranks by royal command, awarded the Knight Grand Cross.
  CONFIRMED.
- **Beirut Pakbara** — Thai Navy SEAL; died Dec 28, 2019, blood infection
  from the operation. CONFIRMED (see correction #3).
- **Narongsak Osatanakorn** — Chiang Rai's former governor, on-the-ground
  operation commander/spokesman; rejected Musk's submersible as
  impractical. CONFIRMED.
- 🧍 **Elon Musk** — built a child-sized submersible ("Wild Boar," from a
  Falcon 9 part); rejected by Narongsak. The subsequent "pedo guy" dispute
  and defamation trial is a real, separate media/legal story — see §6,
  handle per corrections #2.
- 🧍 **Vernon Unsworth** — British caver/expat living locally, dismissed
  Musk's device as a "PR stunt" in a CNN interview; sued Musk for
  defamation; the jury found Musk not liable. Never imply Unsworth was
  found to be what Musk called him — the jury never ruled on that question.

## 2. TIMELINE

- **June 23, 2018:** The 12 boys + Ekkapol enter Tham Luang cave after
  practice; unseasonal monsoon rain flash-floods the lower passages, cutting
  off the exit. Head coach Nopparat raises the alarm that evening.
- **June 24–July 1:** Thai Navy SEALs turn back at the flooded "Pattaya
  Beach" chamber; international specialists arrive and scale up — British
  cave divers, US Air Force personnel (353rd Special Operations Group,
  Kadena, arrived June 28), Australian Federal Police, Chinese teams.
  Commonly cited aggregate: ~17 countries, ~10,000 total contributors over
  the full operation (UNVERIFIED precise headcount, CONFIRMED as the
  standard cited figures).
- **July 2, 2018 (evening):** Volanthen and Stanton find all 13 alive, 9
  days in, roughly 2.5 km inside the flooded system.
- **July 3–5:** Massive pumping operation (see §4); options debated and
  rejected (teach the boys to dive out; wait out the ~4-month monsoon;
  Musk's submersible).
- **July 6:** Saman Kunan dies. Oxygen levels reported falling toward ~15%
  (normal ~19.5–23.5%), adding urgency.
- **July 8–10:** Three-day sedated extraction, 18 divers (13 international +
  5 Thai Navy SEALs), 4 boys per day (July 8/9), final 4 boys + Ekkapol +
  remaining personnel on July 10. **Total elapsed: 18 days.**

## 3. THE ENGINEERING / WATER MANAGEMENT

- Continuous mechanical pumping + diversion of surface streams away from
  the mountain's sinkholes, run in parallel ~2 weeks. **More than one
  billion litres removed total** ("~400 Olympic pools"); peak pump rate
  ~1.6 million litres/hour around July 4. More than 100 exploratory
  boreholes drilled from the surface (UNVERIFIED exact count, CONFIRMED
  general fact). No borehole was ultimately used for extraction — all 13
  came out via the flooded dive route. CONFIRMED throughout.
- Over 100 farmers had ~14,000 rai (~5,500 acres) of rice fields
  deliberately flooded via engineered water diversion to lower the cave's
  water level, with a compensation dispute afterward — a real thread
  **none of the major dramatizations foreground.** CONFIRMED (Nation
  Thailand, Bangkok Post).

## 4. THE EXTRACTION METHOD (sedation and carry)

- Primary source: **Lawthaweesawat C, Harris R, Isara W, Pongpirul K,
  "Prehospital Care of the 13 Hypothermic, Anesthetized Patients in the
  Thailand Cave Rescue," NEJM, DOI 10.1056/NEJMc1900831 (2019)** — the
  closest thing to an official after-action report that exists; authored by
  the actual medical lead (Harris) and Thai military medical officers.
- Sedation: alprazolam 0.5 mg orally, atropine 20 μg/kg IM, ketamine 5 mg/kg
  IM loading dose (ketamine chosen because it preserves breathing drive).
  Each boy fitted with a full-face mask (~80% oxygen) and physically
  carried/towed by a lead diver through the flooded sections. CONFIRMED.
- Extraction order: **decided by the boys themselves inside the cave**,
  before any diver arrived — whoever lived farthest from the cave went
  first, reasoning (Ekkapol's own words, July 18 2018 press conference):
  "we were thinking, when we get out of the cave, we would have to ride the
  bicycle home... so the persons who live the furthest away would be
  allowed to go out first." A secondary, less-sourced claim credits Harris's
  team with a parallel medical-fitness assessment — plausible but not
  cleanly reconciled with the boys' own account; lead with the boys' quote.

## 5. THE 2026 LAOS ECHO — real, confirmed, and NOT a clean win

**CONFIRMED real event:** May 19–20, 2026, eight villagers/artisanal gold
miners entered a flooded mine/cave in Xaysomboun Province, Laos; one
escaped, seven trapped. **Mikko Paasi** (Finnish, Thailand-based) and
**Norraseth "Nut" Palasri** (Thai; also transliterated "Norrased Palasing")
— both veterans of the 2018 Tham Luang rescue — led the international
diving effort (~100 rescuers, 2026-05-27 to 06-06). Five survivors located
May 27–29 (one extracted underwater May 29; four self-extracted May 30 as
water dropped); **two miners were never located** — a cave-in plus a 4.2
magnitude earthquake made the site too unstable, and the search was
formally called off June 6, 2026. Sources: CBS News (May 26 & follow-up),
CNN (May 28–31), Al Jazeera (June 6), ABC News Australia, UPI, The Scuba
News.

## 6. THE MUSK–UNSWORTH DEFAMATION CASE — full, careful chronology

Case: *Unsworth v. Musk*, No. 2:18-cv-08048 (C.D. Cal., Judge Stephen V.
Wilson). **Civil, not criminal** — say "not liable," never "not guilty" or
"cleared."

1. July 8, 2018: Musk tweets the mini-sub is en route.
2. July 10: Rescue completed without it.
3. July 13: Unsworth calls it a "PR stunt" in a CNN interview.
4. July 15: Musk tweets "Sorry pedo guy, you really did ask for it"
   (doesn't name Unsworth); deletes it, apologizes.
5. Later July/Aug 2018: In emails to BuzzFeed's Ryan Mac, Musk goes further
   — "child rapist," an unfounded "child bride" claim. Mac had not agreed
   to "off the record"; BuzzFeed published.
6. Sept 17, 2018: Unsworth sues for defamation.
7. Nov 20, 2019: Judge Wilson rules Unsworth was **not a public figure** for
   these statements — meaning the lower negligence standard, not the higher
   "actual malice" standard, applied.
8. Dec 3–6, 2019: Four-day jury trial, LA. Musk testifies and apologizes on
   the stand. The BuzzFeed emails are admitted **only** as state-of-mind
   evidence — the judge instructed the jury they could not be an
   independent basis for the verdict. Unsworth sought $190M.
9. Dec 6, 2019: Eight-person jury deliberates under an hour; finds Musk
   **not liable**, zero damages. Per named juror Carl Shusterman's account
   to BuzzFeed, the panel's reasoning centered on whether a reasonable
   reader could tell the un-named tweet referred to Unsworth specifically —
   they never reached the truth/falsity question.

**Safest script language (use near-verbatim):** *"Musk apologized for the
tweet and deleted it within days. Unsworth sued for defamation anyway. In
December 2019, a Los Angeles jury heard both sides — including Musk's own
apology on the stand — and found him not liable. It was a civil verdict,
not a criminal one, and by at least one juror's account it turned on a
narrow technical question — whether the tweet could be shown to refer to
Unsworth by name — not on whether the underlying accusation was ever true.
Unsworth's case ended there, with no damages awarded, but also no ruling
from either court or jury that Musk's original words had been accurate."*
Never restate the "child rapist"/"child bride" claims as fact anywhere —
they are Musk's unproven, disputed allegations, never adjudicated.

## 7. DUANGPHET "DOM" PHROMTHEP — 2023 death (highest-sensitivity thread)

- Team captain, one of the 12 rescued boys. Won a football scholarship to
  Brooke House College, Market Harborough, Leicestershire, England
  (enrolled ~Aug 2022).
- Found unconscious **Sunday, Feb 12, 2023** in his dormitory; died
  **Tuesday, Feb 14, 2023**, at Kettering General Hospital, aged 17, having
  never regained consciousness.
- Inquest (Leicester Coroner's Court) concluded **Oct 4, 2023**. Coroner
  Prof. Catherine Mason's exact recorded language: *"Mr Phromthep was not
  known to mental health services, and it is not known why he took the
  actions that he did... It could not have been foreseen or prevented. The
  police investigation has found no evidence of third-party involvement or
  suspicious circumstances."* Conclusion: **suicide.**
- No credible reporting ties the death to bullying, academic pressure, or
  the 2018 cave trauma — only unverified social-media speculation exists,
  which must not be repeated or lent credibility.
- His mother requested Buddhist funeral rites be performed in England "so
  his spirit is not trapped where he died"; Thailand's ambassador attended
  the UK service; a Thailand memorial followed.

**Real-outlet model for responsible handling:** lead with the coroner's
official, dated legal conclusion rather than dwelling on 2023's initial
rumor/ambiguity; quote the coroner's own "unforeseeable/unknown" language
(protects against implying blame); never describe method; keep the focus
on tribute/grief, not the cause-of-death reveal as a hook. Khaosod
English's own framing ("No Matter Dom's Cause of Death, The Family Is Still
Devastated") is the right editorial model — the "how" is secondary to
honoring the person.

**⚠ EDITORIAL GATE, not just a caution:** both the primary research and an
independent adversarial fact-check on this thread converged on the same
recommendation: **whether to include this thread at all is a go/no-go
decision for Akshay**, not a soft caveat to work around. This has zero
mystery content (the cause is officially, finally settled) — its only
legitimate role is a brief, respectful epilogue/tribute beat about what
happened to the Wild Boars after 2018, never a "twist" or curiosity-gap
reveal. Per the channel's own precedent (WTC/United 93 dedicated ethics
review), this should be treated the same way before it's scripted at all.

## 9. THE COVERAGE GAP — what to lead with instead of a recap

Three major treatments already exist and compete over the same real
estate: **Thirteen Lives** (2022, Ron Howard, dramatized — the divers'
heroics; explicitly cut the Musk feud), **The Rescue** (2021, Nat Geo,
documentary, 96% RT — real footage + diver interviews, but the boys
themselves don't appear because Netflix bought exclusive life rights; also
explicitly cut Musk), **Netflix's Thai Cave Rescue** (2022, scripted
6-episode series — the boys'/coach's perspective, weakest reviews of the
three, 63% RT).

**Genuinely open threads none of them lead with:**
1. **The Musk trial itself** — both other productions confirm on record
   they cut it; the actual federal defamation case with real testimony has
   never been the subject of a documentary treatment.
2. **The credit dispute** — freelance divers Ben Reymenants and Maksym
   Polejaka reportedly located the group first; Stanton/Volanthen (who
   every screen version lionizes) didn't officially credit them until three
   years later.
3. **The statelessness story** — foregrounded nowhere: Ekkapol and several
   boys were stateless Tai Lue minority residents; Thailand fast-tracked
   citizenship grants within weeks, real international news at the time.
4. **The farmers** — over 100 farmers had ~5,500 acres deliberately flooded;
   near-absent from all three productions.
5. **Beirut Pakbara's delayed death** — sits awkwardly between the
   productions' timelines (Dec 2019, after all coverage had moved on) and
   is missing from the pop-culture record entirely.
6. **The life-rights auction itself** — multiple studios raced to buy rights
   to vulnerable, in some cases stateless, minors within days of rescue;
   Netflix's exclusive buy is what blocked Nat Geo's Oscar-nominated team
   from ever speaking to the boys — a real ethics story, never itself
   documented.

**Recommended spine:** an evidence-forward documentary (mindwired's own
proven format) that tells the rescue accurately but leads its differentiation
with the threads above — particularly the Musk trial (real court record,
zero mystery-content risk, completely unexploited) and the statelessness/
farmers threads (real, sourced, never centered) — closing on the honest,
still-unfolding 2026 Laos echo as a coda, not the hook.
