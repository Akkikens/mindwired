### Act 5 — The glide-vs-dive debate (fresh technical ground, unresolved in Videos 1-2)

**Official/mainstream case — uncontrolled, high-speed final dive:**
- **[CONFIRMED primary]** Ian Holland (DSTG), "MH370 Burst Frequency Offset
  Analysis and Implications on Descent Rate at End-of-Flight," IEEE 2017 —
  [arXiv:1702.02432](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.02432). Modeling the
  aircraft's own known Doppler-compensation noise band against the final two
  partial SATCOM transmissions (00:19:29 and 00:19:37 UTC — the aircraft's
  own automatic attempt to log back on after fuel exhaustion): descent rate
  roughly 2,900-14,800 fpm at the first point, roughly 13,800-25,300 fpm just
  8 seconds later — doubling in 8 seconds, an average downward acceleration
  of about 0.68g. A normal descent is ~2,000 fpm; these are 7-13× that.
- **[CONFIRMED]** The final, incomplete 7th handshake at 08:19:29 MYT is
  itself evidence of engine flameout: when the engines stop, the APU
  auto-starts and re-powers the SATCOM just long enough to force one last
  involuntary log-on attempt, before the APU's own fuel runs out minutes
  later. Source: ATSB analysis summarized at
  [mh370.radiantphysics.com](https://mh370.radiantphysics.com/2018/08/19/end-of-flight-simulations-of-mh370/).
- **[CONFIRMED]** The flap/flaperon rear-spar rivet-line damage on the
  recovered right outboard flap and the Réunion flaperon only geometrically
  lines up when the flap is in the RETRACTED position — i.e. cruise
  configuration, not set up for a landing/ditching.
  [FlightGlobal](https://www.flightglobal.com/mh370-damage-analysis-indicates-flaps-not-deployed/122163.article) ·
  [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/malaysia-airlines-flight-370-missing-plane-mh370-flaperon-no-pilot/).
  ATSB's Peter Foley adds a plain-logic argument defending this against
  challenge: there's no reason to deliberately run the tanks dry and then
  attempt an unpowered glide when ditching under power, while fuel remained,
  was always available — an intentional glide-to-extend-range scenario
  doesn't even fit its own internal logic.
  [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2018/05/22/australia-investigators-defend-mh370-out-of-control-scenario/1633687).
- **[CONFIRMED]** No large, intact debris sections were ever found — only
  small, fragmented pieces, consistent with a high-energy, high-speed,
  closer-to-vertical impact rather than a controlled ditching (which tends
