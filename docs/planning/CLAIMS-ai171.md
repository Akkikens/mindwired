## ⚠ DATA CORRECTIONS (baked in — do NOT revert)

1. **Death toll = 260 total** (230 passengers + 12 crew aboard, 241 of 242 aboard
   died + 19 on the ground). Early wire reports cited lower/rounding figures —
   use 260 as the settled total, only cite lower numbers if explicitly framed as
   "in the immediate hours after the crash."
2. **NOT "India's deadliest air disaster ever."** That remains the **1996 Charkhi
   Dadri mid-air collision** (~349-350 dead). AI171 is **Air India's own deadliest
   crash** and **India's second-deadliest aviation disaster**, the worst in ~30
   years. Get this exact framing right — "deadliest ever" is factually wrong.
3. **First fatal hull-loss of a Boeing 787** since the type entered service in
   2011 — well-sourced, safe, strong claim (Bloomberg, Simple Flying, Reuters).
4. Engines are **General Electric GEnx-1B70**, NOT Rolls-Royce Trent.
5. **Only a PRELIMINARY report exists** (published 12 July 2025). It is, by
   design, a facts-only document — it explicitly does NOT assign cause or blame.
   The **final report is not due until October 2026** per AAIB's own statement to
   India's Supreme Court (July 2026). Never present the preliminary report's
   contents as a concluded "cause."
6. **The CVR exchange, exactly as AAIB published it:** one pilot asks why the
   other "cut off" the fuel; the second denies doing so. **AAIB's own published
   text does NOT name which pilot said which line** — any attribution of specific
   words to Sabharwal vs. Kunder by name comes from unofficial secondary
   reporting, not the primary document. Treat as UNVERIFIED-by-primary-source.
7. **WHY the switches moved is unresolved and actively disputed — present BOTH
   readings with equal weight, neither as fact:**
   - Reading A (reported in some international press): consistent with
     deliberate pilot action — **not an AAIB conclusion.**
   - Reading B (Federation of Indian Pilots): an electrical fault/anomaly caused
     the switches to trip as a downstream symptom, not pilot input — also **not
     an AAIB conclusion**, and FIP's claim of "known prior electrical issues" on
     this specific airframe is UNVERIFIED, pending AAIB's own maintenance review.
8. **🧍 HIGH SENSITIVITY — Captain Sabharwal's father, Pushkar Raj Sabharwal (91,
   living)**, has publicly disputed how investigators characterized his son,
   called the reading "selective," and taken the matter to the Supreme Court.
   **🧍 Two pilot unions (ICPA, ALPA-India)** have called "pilot suicide" framing
   "reckless and unfounded" and said the crew "deserve respect, not unfounded
   character judgement." Any narration referencing the cockpit exchange MUST be
   paired with: this is contested, the family disputes it, the investigation is
   incomplete.
9. **A 2018 FAA bulletin** (advisory, non-mandatory) flagged that some 787
   fuel-control-switch locking features had been found disengaged — Air India
   never inspected under it (the bulletin wasn't mandatory). The FAA/Boeing later
   stated the switch design itself is safe. **Do not claim a "known faulty
   switch design caused this crash"** — unresolved whether THIS aircraft's
   switches were ever inspected.
10. **🧍 Sole survivor: Vishwash Kumar Ramesh** (British citizen, seat 11A, living,
    identifiable, has spoken publicly about PTSD). Use ONLY his own published,
    on-record quotes (BBC/CBS). No invented interior monologue, no speculation
    about his mental state beyond what he's said, no dramatized reenactment
    presented as literal fact.
11. **Litigation:** ONE confirmed suit — filed 16 Sept 2025, Delaware Superior
    Court, by families of 4 passengers, against Boeing and Honeywell (maker of
    the fuel switch), alleging negligence in the switch's locking mechanism.
    Do NOT overstate as a broad "families sue" class action — it's 4 families.
    No other confirmed India/UK litigation found — treat any claim of "multiple
    lawsuits" as unverified.
12. **No fleet-wide 787 grounding occurred** anywhere (India, US, EU) — DGCA
    ordered airline-specific enhanced inspections on Air India's 787 fleet only.
    In Feb 2026, DGCA reviewed and ultimately **cleared** the fuel-switch design
    rather than mandating a fleet fix.

## FLIGHT / AIRCRAFT / CREW / SURVIVOR

- **[CONFIRMED]** AI171, Ahmedabad → London Gatwick, **12 June 2025**, departed
  13:38 IST; crashed **32 seconds after takeoff**, ~1.7km from the runway, into
  the hostel buildings of **B.J. Medical College**, Meghaninagar. Boeing 787-8,
  registration **VT-ANB**, delivered Jan 2014 (~12 years old, ~41,868 airframe
  hours), engines re-installed <3 months before the crash.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Crew: **Captain Sumeet Sabharwal** (56, ~15,600 total hours,
  ~8,600 on-787, pilot monitoring) and **First Officer Clive Kunder** (32, ~3,400
  total hours, ~1,100 on-787, pilot flying). Both deceased.
- **[CONFIRMED]** 242 souls aboard (230 pax + 12 crew): 169 Indian, 53 British, 7
  Portuguese, 1 Canadian. 241 of 242 aboard died + 19 on the ground = **260
  total** (correction #1). 🧍 **Sole survivor: Vishwash Kumar Ramesh**, seat 11A
  (correction #10) — his brother Ajay Ramesh, seated nearby, was among the dead.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Real airport CCTV footage of the takeoff/crash exists, verified
  by AP; shows normal liftoff, failure to gain altitude, gear still down,
  sag/descent, impact ~32s after liftoff. A separate bystander phone video (shot
  by a 17-year-old, Aryan Asari) also captured the final descent. **Neither is
  free-use** (see footage section below).

## THE INVESTIGATION

- **[CONFIRMED]** AAIB preliminary report, published **12 July 2025** (~30 days
  post-crash, meets ICAO Annex 13's benchmark). Real public-record document —
  usable as an exhibit: `aaib.gov.in/What's New Assets/Preliminary Report
  VT-ANB.pdf`.
- **[CONFIRMED — exact finding]** Both fuel-control switches moved from **RUN to
  CUTOFF one second apart**, ~3 seconds after liftoff (~08:08:42 IST, ~180kt).
  CVR: one pilot asks why the other cut off fuel; the other denies it
  (correction #6 — no named attribution in the primary document). RAT deployed
  ~5s after cutoff. Switches were moved back to RUN ~10s after cutoff; an
  auto-relight sequence began but engines couldn't recover core speed before
  impact. Total cutoff-to-impact: **~29 seconds**.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Final report **not due until October 2026** per AAIB's own
  statement to India's Supreme Court (July 2026) — the Court also **declined to
  order release of the CVR transcript**. The investigation remains genuinely
  open (correction #5).
- **[DISPUTED — present both, neither as fact]** Why the switches moved
  (correction #7): pilot-action reading (reported by some outlets, one named
  aviation YouTuber/commentator "Captain Steve" on CNN-News18 has stated this
  opinion — label as HIS opinion, not a finding) vs. Federation of Indian
  Pilots' electrical-fault-symptom counter-theory.
- **[CONFIRMED / 🧍 high sensitivity]** Sabharwal's father disputes the
  characterization (correction #8); ICPA and ALPA-India have publicly pushed
  back on "pilot suicide" framing as "reckless and unfounded" / demanding the
  crew be treated with "respect, not unfounded character judgement."
- **[CONFIRMED]** 2018 FAA bulletin on 787 fuel-switch locking mechanisms —
  advisory only, Air India never inspected under it; FAA/Boeing later called the
  design itself safe (correction #9).

## AFTERMATH

- **[CONFIRMED]** Air India paid **₹25 lakh (~$30,000) interim compensation**
  per family (96% of families); Tata Sons separately announced **₹1 crore
  (~$120,000) ex-gratia** per family (🧍 announced by Chairman N. Chandrasekaran)
  and set up a ₹500 crore "AI171 Memorial and Welfare Trust."
- **[CONFIRMED]** 🧍 Air India CEO **Campbell Wilson** called the crash
  "devastating," warned against "premature conclusions" after the preliminary
  report — noting it "identified no cause nor made any recommendations."
- **[CONFIRMED]** Litigation: ONE suit, Delaware Superior Court, 16 Sept 2025, 4
  passenger families vs. Boeing + Honeywell (correction #11).
- **[CONFIRMED]** No fleet-wide grounding (correction #12); DGCA issued 9
  "show cause" notices to Air India over 6 months for 5 safety violations
  (🧍 per Minister Ram Mohan Naidu, parliamentary statement).
- **[CONFIRMED]** 🧍 UK PM Keir Starmer, Foreign Secretary David Lammy, and King
  Charles III issued condolence statements (53 British nationals aboard). 🧍 PM
  Modi visited the site day-of; ordered a "high-level probe."
- **[CONFIRMED]** One-year anniversary (12 June 2026): commemoration at B.J.
  Medical College, 260 trees planted (one per victim), attended by families,
  officials, foreign dignitaries.

## ARCHIVAL / FOOTAGE PLAN — FUNDAMENTALLY DIFFERENT FROM PRIOR EPISODES

**No free crash footage exists.** Both the CCTV clip (AP/Reuters-distributed,
explicitly kept OFF Wikimedia Commons by its own editors with a "do not copy to
Commons" warning — India's copyright law does not share the US's narrow view of
CCTV authorship) and the bystander phone video (a private citizen's copyrighted
recording, licensed to news outlets under news-reporting terms) are unambiguously
rights-controlled. **Do not use either.**

**What IS real and usable:**
- **AAIB Preliminary Report PDF** — real government exhibit, same pattern as
  BEA/CAIB in prior episodes.
- **A handful of GODL-India-licensed PMO/PIB photos** on Wikimedia Commons
  (PM Modi's site visit, wreckage aftermath) — genuinely open (GODL-India is a
  real worldwide royalty-free license with attribution), confirmed via the file
  page's explicit license tag. **Do NOT assume other Indian-government footage
  is PD by default** — India's copyright law defaults to 60-year Government
  Copyright unless a specific open license (like GODL-India) is attached, unlike
  US federal works.
- **Generic Boeing 787 Dreamliner b-roll** (the aircraft type, not this specific
  tail) via the standard `fetch_footage.py` pipeline (Pexels/Pixabay) — eyeball
  the contact sheet, don't assume hits are accurate.
- Ahmedabad/Gujarat establishing shots — likely thin, verify individually.

**Industry precedent (validates the approach):** Wyngx's well-received video used
a **full custom animated reconstruction**, not the real clips, as its visual

precedent for unfilmable/rights-constrained moments (labeled clearly as
