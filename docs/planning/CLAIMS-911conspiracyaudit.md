## ITEM 3 — THE SPINE: the air-quality statements

### CONFIRMED — what was actually said
EPA press release, **18 September 2001**, quoting the EPA Administrator
(Christine Todd Whitman 🧍):
> "I am glad to reassure the people of New York and Washington, D.C. that their
> air is safe to breath [sic] . . ."

CONFIRMED — PDF p.19. The `[sic]` is the IG's own.

Related headings the IG lists on the same page, all CONFIRMED:
- 21 Sep 2001: "NYC Monitoring Efforts Continue to Show Safe Drinking Water & Air"
- 03 Oct 2001: "Data Confirms No Significant Public Health Risks; Rescue Crews and
  Nearby Residents Should Take Appropriate Precautions. . ."

### CONFIRMED — the finding (this is the episode's single most important line)
> "...it did not have sufficient data and analyses to make such a blanket
> statement."

CONFIRMED — PDF p.5, Executive Summary. **This exact fragment is the locked
thumbnail (variant A), so per the packaging handoff it must appear on screen
inside the first 30 seconds.** Full surrounding sentence, same page: EPA's early
public statements "reassured the public regarding the safety of the air outside
the Ground Zero area," and the IG found the data did not support that.

And PDF p.19:
> "Information and the analyses of available data did not fully support the
> statement made in the September 18, 2001, release, which quoted the EPA
> Administrator as saying the air was 'safe' to breathe."

### CONFIRMED — the White House role
PDF p.5:
> "The White House Council on Environmental Quality influenced, through the
> collaboration process, the information that EPA communicated to the public
> through its early press releases when it convinced EPA to add reassuring
> statements and delete cautionary ones."

PDF p.26, on the draft dated 14 Sep issued 16 Sep 2001:
> "Every change that was suggested by the CEQ contact was made. The CEQ official's
> suggested changes added reassuring statements and deleted cautionary statements."

The specific sentence deleted and never replaced (PDF p.26) — use this verbatim,
it is the most concrete beat in the whole item:
> "The concern raised by these samples would be for the workers at the cleanup
> site and for those workers who might be returning to their offices on or near
> Water Street on Monday, September 17, 2001."

PDF p.25, a **12 September 2001** e-mail from the EPA Deputy Administrator's Chief
of Staff to senior EPA officials:
> "All statements to the media should be cleared through the NSC [National
> Security Council] before they are released."

Same page: the Associate Administrator for EPA's Office of Communications said
**no press release could be issued for a 3-to-4-week period after September 11
without approval from the CEQ contact.** CONFIRMED.

PDF p.27 — the ownership exchange, all CONFIRMED:
- EPA Chief of Staff, asked if she could claim ownership of the early WTC press
  releases: she could not, "because the ownership was joint ownership between EPA
  and the White House," and "final approval came from the White House."
- She also said the desire to **reopen Wall Street** and national security concerns
  were considered when preparing the early releases.
- The OCEMR Associate Administrator on the 16 September release: **"I did not feel
  like it was my press release."**

### CONFIRMED — what was in the dust
PDF p.24: **over 25 percent** of the bulk dust samples EPA had collected and
analysed **by 18 September** showed asbestos above the 1 percent threshold EPA uses
to indicate significant risk. During September 2001 EPA and NYC monitoring recorded
**30 exceedances** of the AHERA standard (70 s/mm²); Oct 2001–May 2002, seven.
The IG notes the 1 percent figure is **not a health-based standard** and dust below
it "could pose a health risk" — do not overstate it.

### ⚠ CONFIRMED — the balancing finding (MANDATORY, do not omit)
PDF p.19:
> "...we found no evidence that EPA attempted to conceal data results from the
> public."

EPA published monitoring data on its public website with interactive maps. **Script
this.** The honest story is a government that published its data while its
messaging was being edited for reassurance by the White House — which is more
damning and more defensible than "they hid it."

### CONFIRMED — the limitation on the IG's own work
PDF p.26: "our analysis of CEQ's input was limited because CEQ officials chose not
to meet with us." State this.

### 🧍 Living-person handling
Christine Todd Whitman is living. Quote the press release and the IG's findings;
do **not** assert she lied or knew. The IG did not find that, and the distinction
is the whole difference between this episode and a truther video.

## ITEM 2 — NORAD's timeline: the accounts were wrong, and the Commission said so

**Primary source, now in hand:** *The 9/11 Commission Report*, official GPO
edition, 585pp, public domain — `out/thumbs/_src/gpo_911report.pdf`
(govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GPO-911REPORT). Cites are **PDF p.52 = printed p.34**,
verified by extraction.

### CONFIRMED — verbatim
> "More than the actual events, inaccurate government accounts of those events
> made it appear that the military was notified in time to respond to two of the
> hijackings, raising questions about the adequacy of the response."

> "In public testimony before this Commission in May 2003, NORAD officials stated
> that at 9:16, NEADS received hijack notification of United 93 from the FAA.
> **This statement was incorrect.** There was no hijack to report at 9:16."

> "In this same public testimony, NORAD officials stated that at 9:24, NEADS
> received notification of the hijacking of American 77. **This statement was
> also incorrect.**"

> "In their testimony and in other public accounts, NORAD officials also stated
> that the Langley fighters were scrambled to respond to the notifications about
> American 77, United 93, or both. **These statements were incorrect as well.**"

> "Yet this response to a phantom aircraft was not recounted in a single public
> timeline or statement issued by the FAA or Department of Defense."

The Commission says it three times in its own words. It needs no embellishment
and must not get any.

## ITEM 4 — the bin Laden family flights

**Primary source:** same Commission report, **PDF p.347 = printed p.329-330**,
section "Flights of Saudi Nationals Leaving the United States". The Commission
posed three questions and answered all three.

### CONFIRMED — verbatim
> "First, we found no evidence that any flights of Saudi nationals, domestic or
> international, took place before the reopening of national airspace on the
> morning of September 13, 2001. To the contrary, every flight we have identified
> occurred after national airspace reopened."

> "Second, we found no evidence of political intervention."

> "Third, we believe that the FBI conducted a satisfactory screening of Saudi
> nationals who left the United States on charter flights."

Also CONFIRMED, same pages: Richard Clarke asked the FBI's Dale Watson "to handle
that... And if they had no objections, it would be fine with me," and added "I
have no recollection of clearing it with anybody at the White House." The
President and Vice President told the Commission they were unaware of the issue
until it surfaced in the media.

### How to play it — this is the PARTIAL, and it is load-bearing
The flights are real: that part of the rumour was **true**, and conceding it is
why the audience trusts the other three items. But the sinister readings — that
they flew while airspace was closed, or that the White House arranged it — are
**specifically what the Commission looked for and did not find**. Label it PARTIAL
on screen and state both halves in the same breath.
