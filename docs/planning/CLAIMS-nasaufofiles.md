## THE REAL STORY (facts for the script, organized by act)

### Act 1 — The report that started this episode
- **[CONFIRMED primary]** NASA Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Independent
  Study Team Report, released Sept 14, 2023. 16-member team (chair: Dr. David
  Spergel, astrophysicist; members include former NASA astronaut Scott Kelly,
  FAA officials, aerospace/data-science experts, science journalist Nadia
  Drake) commissioned June 2022 — explicitly NOT a re-investigation of past
  incidents, but a roadmap for how NASA's tools/data could help going forward.
- **[CONFIRMED primary]** One public deliberation meeting (May 31, 2023),
  8 formal charge questions, unclassified/open data only. Recommends NASA
  lean on Earth-observing satellites, commercial remote-sensing, NEXRAD
  weather radar, AI/ML, and the FAA's Aviation Safety Reporting System
  (~100,000 reports/year).
- **[CONFIRMED primary, verbatim]** *"It is increasingly clear that the
  majority of UAP observations can be attributed to known phenomena or
  occurrences"* — and the obstacle to resolving the rest is data quality:
  *"the data needed to explain these anomalous sightings often do not
  exist."* Not confirmed anomalies — insufficient data.
- **[CONFIRMED primary]** The report frames the whole question around
  airspace safety and sensor-data quality: *"the threat to U.S. airspace
  safety posed by UAP is self-evident."* Never an alien-contact framing.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Same day, NASA created a new "Director of UAP Research"
  role, naming Mark McInerney (NASA's former DoD liaison) to it (correction
  #10 on his current status).

### Act 2 — The Tic Tac (2004)
- **[CONFIRMED, attributed eyewitness testimony]** Nov 14, 2004, ~100 miles
  off San Diego. USS Nimitz Carrier Strike Group; cruiser USS Princeton
  (radar operator Kevin Day) tracked anomalous contacts for ~2 weeks before
  vectoring two VFA-41 "Black Aces" F/A-18F Super Hornets (Cmdr. David
  Fravor, lead; then-Lt. Alex Dietrich, wingman) to investigate.
- **[CONFIRMED, attributed]** Both pilots describe a ~40-foot white "Tic
  Tac"-shaped object, no wings, no visible engine/exhaust, hovering over
  disturbed white water, mirroring Fravor's aircraft before accelerating away
  in seconds. Princeton radar reportedly reacquired a contact ~60 miles away
  shortly after.
- **[CONFIRMED, direct quotes]** Fravor, ABC News: *"I think it was not from
  this world."* Fravor, sworn House testimony: the object was "far superior
  to anything that we had at the time, have today, or are looking to develop
  in the next 10 to 20 years." Dietrich, 60 Minutes: *"It was unidentified.
  And that's why it was so unsettling to us... we couldn't classify it."*
  Dietrich has separately urged against "racing to conclusions" (correction
  #5 — use the contrast).
- **[CONFIRMED]** ~90 minutes later, a separate aircrew (Lt. Cmdr. Chad
  Underwood) recorded ~76 seconds of infrared video — this became the
  publicly released "FLIR1" clip. It is real sensor footage of the aftermath/
  a related contact, not literally the visual encounter Fravor/Dietrich
  described.

### Act 3 — GIMBAL and GOFAST (2015) and the official release
- **[CONFIRMED]** Both recorded January 2015 by VFA-11 "Red Rippers" F/A-18F
  Super Hornets (ATFLIR pods), USS Theodore Roosevelt Carrier Strike Group,

- **[CONFIRMED]** All three videos (FLIR1, GIMBAL, GOFAST) had circulated via

- **[DISPUTED, attributed — the honest counterweight]** Mick West (Metabunk)

### Act 4 — Grusch, the 2023 hearing, and the official rebuttal
- **[CONFIRMED]** July 26, 2023, House Oversight Committee hearing,
  "Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena: Implications on National Security,
  Public Safety, and Government Transparency." Three sworn witnesses: David
