## The Disaster

- **[CONFIRMED]** TWA Flight 800, a Boeing 747-131 (N93119), took off from
  JFK Runway 22R at 8:19 p.m. EDT on a scheduled JFK-to-Paris (Charles de
  Gaulle) run.
  — NTSB AAR-00/03: https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/AAR0003.pdf
- **[CONFIRMED]** The last ATC exchange began at 8:30:15 p.m. (Boston
  Center: "TWA eight hundred climb and maintain one five thousand");
  Kevorkian acknowledged ("climb thrust" / "climb to one five thousand"),
  Snyder read back the clearance, and the flight engineer reported
  "power's set" at 8:30:35 p.m. The CVR recorded a brief mechanical/
  tape-type sound at 8:31:05 p.m. and terminated at 8:31:12 p.m.; NTSB
  determined the final fraction of a second of usable tape held a loud,
  explosive transient (audible on slowed playback) consistent with the
  onset of in-flight breakup. The FAA Trevose, PA radar site's last
  transponder return also came at 8:31:12 p.m. — roughly 12 minutes after
  takeoff, about 8 miles south of East Moriches, Long Island, at
  approximately 13,700 ft.
  — NTSB AAR-00/03 CVR group factual report/timeline; CVR transcript
  mirrored at https://tailstrike.com/database/17-july-1996-twa-800.
  This is the strongest documented "final transmission before the
  explosion" beat — completely routine ATC chatter, ending mid-sequence.
  Do not embellish with dialogue not in the transcript. See Correction #1.
- **[CONFIRMED]** The center wing fuel tank (CWT) explosion caused the
  nose section (cockpit, upper deck, first cabin rows) to separate from
  the fuselage within about 5 seconds; the remainder of the aircraft
  continued flying/climbing further before falling into the Atlantic,
  trailing fire. (A specific extra-altitude figure for the post-separation
  climb — e.g., "~3,000 ft higher" — appears in some secondary sources;
  verify against the primary report before quoting a number.)
  — NTSB AAR-00/03 sequence-of-events/wreckage-distribution analysis.
- **[CONFIRMED]** The debris field divided into three zones along the
  flight path: the "red zone" (closest to the explosion point, forward
  fuselage/CWT debris, light sooting only), the "yellow zone" (containing
  the separated nose section), and the "green zone" (main fuselage, wings,
  engines — furthest along the flight path).
  — NTSB AAR-00/03 wreckage-distribution analysis; corroborated by Admiral
  Cloudberg's independent writeup. Useful for the visual reconstruction
  beat; cite NTSB as primary.
- **[CONFIRMED]** A nearby Eastwind Airlines Boeing 737 pilot reported
  seeing an explosion around 16,000 ft and the aircraft going down into
  the water; numerous other pilots and ground witnesses in the area also
  reported an explosion/fireball and falling debris.
  — NTSB witness-group factual reports. Do not name the Eastwind captain
  unless his name appears directly in the NTSB witness report — not from a
  secondary blog.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Dozens of ground/air witnesses (exact tallies vary — see
  Correction #4) described seeing a streak of light rising toward the
  aircraft in the seconds before the explosion/fireball, which many
  interpreted at the time as a missile.
  — NTSB witness group study; FBI witness canvass.
- **[CONFIRMED, officially — DISPUTED by named critics]** The NTSB's
  official conclusion, supported by a CIA-produced explanatory
  video/animation presented at a November 1997 FBI press conference, is
  that the "streak of light" witnesses saw was the aircraft itself —
  post-explosion, trailing burning jet fuel while continuing to climb on
  residual momentum before descending — not a missile. This is disputed on
  technical grounds by a dissenting member of the FBI's own missile-
  evidence team (per reporting), by a March 1999 internal CIA memo that
  put the reconstructed max altitude at 14,500 ft rather than the ~17,000
  ft the released animation depicted, and by physicist Tom Stalcup. See
  Correction #5 — frame as "the official explanation, disputed by some
  witnesses/critics," never as an unchallenged rebuttal.
  — NTSB AAR-00/03 witness-evidence analysis; CIA video (NBC Learn

  ; Tom Stalcup's technical rebuttal: https://tomstalcup.com/flight-800-didnt-crash-like-a-missile/
- **[DISPUTED — attributed to advocacy site, not government]** A claim
  that the CIA/NTSB streak-of-light explanation is "inconsistent with 94%
  of the relevant eyewitnesses."
  — flight800.org (a site explicitly organized around contesting the
  official finding): https://flight800.org/eye_old.html — do NOT present
  this 94% figure as fact; attribute by name if used at all, paired
  immediately with the NTSB/CIA position.
- **[CONFIRMED]** More than 95% of the aircraft's structure, and the

## The Official Investigation & Cause

- **[CONFIRMED — the single most important claim in the episode]** NTSB's
  final report (adopted August 23, 2000) determined the probable cause of
  the accident was: an explosion of the center wing fuel tank (CWT),
  resulting from ignition of a flammable fuel/air vapor mixture in the
  tank. The exact ignition source could not be determined with certainty,
  but of the sources evaluated, the most likely was a short circuit
  outside the CWT that allowed excessive voltage to enter the tank
  through wiring associated with the fuel quantity indication system
  (FQIS).
  — NTSB Aircraft Accident Report AAR-00/03, Probable Cause section:
  https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/AAR0003.pdf
  Quote/cite close to verbatim with an exact page citation once the full
  PDF text is pulled directly. **Preserve NTSB's own hedge language**
  ("most likely," "could not be determined with certainty") — do not
  flatten it into unqualified certainty; this hedge is itself sometimes
  weaponized by conspiracy theorists as "NTSB doesn't really know," which
  the script should acknowledge rather than obscure.
- **[CONFIRMED]** FAA wiring-separation regulations at the time did not
  explicitly require FQIS wiring to be routed separately from other
  high-power wiring; Boeing had not provided such protection on the 747,
  and NTSB found FQIS wiring was frequently bundled in common raceways
  with cabin-lighting and other system wiring.
  — NTSB AAR-00/03 findings, as summarized by Wikibooks "Understanding Air
  Safety in the Jet Age" and Admiral Cloudberg's long-form writeup:
  https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Understanding_Air_Safety_in_the_Jet_Age/Bad_Design,_Bad_Maintenance_-_TWA_800
  — verify final phrasing against the NTSB PDF before a direct on-screen
  citation card.
- **[CONFIRMED]** A contributing factor NTSB identified was the design and
  certification standards for the 747 (and transport aircraft generally)
  that allowed heat sources (e.g., air-conditioning packs) located
  beneath/adjacent to the CWT without a requirement to reduce heat
  transferred into the tank or render the fuel vapor non-flammable,
  permitting flammable fuel/air vapor to exist in the CWT during normal
  operations.
  — NTSB AAR-00/03, Findings/Contributing Factors section.
- **[CONFIRMED]** Prior to departure, ground crew locked out the No. 3
  engine thrust reverser due to sensor problems and replaced severed
  cables; during refueling, maintenance personnel manually overrode the
  automatic Volumetric Shutoff (VSO) control by pulling the volumetric
  fuse and an overflow circuit breaker; the aircraft had numerous
  VSO-related maintenance write-ups in the weeks before the accident.
  — NTSB AAR-00/03, per a detailed secondary summary (Wikipedia) of the
  report. **Do not conflate this VSO/refueling maintenance thread with the
  CWT ignition-source finding — they are separate mechanical threads in
  the report.** Verify against the primary PDF before stating as
  on-screen fact, since this came via a secondary aggregator this pass.
- **[UNVERIFIED — belongs to the 2026 FOIA wave, not the cause finding]**
  A theory that the crash was caused by a stray U.S. Navy missile fired
  during a nearby exercise/test — see Conspiracy Theories section. NTSB
  and FBI both affirmatively excluded a missile strike based on the
  evidence they examined (CONFIRMED above); the missile theory itself
  remains ALLEGED by named advocates/plaintiffs only.
