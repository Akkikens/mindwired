# Channel-Health Checklist — surviving the 2026 "inauthentic content" wave

Run this against **every episode before upload** (all channels; most acute for
faceless: Black Box Breakdown, mindwired, DimaagBatti). Born from the 2026
YouTube demonetization wave targeting *mass production / templated / no-human-
fingerprint* content — Gemini scans the whole channel, not one video. Our
existing anti-slop work (footage-first, sketch/mascot brand, cloned voice, VO
humanization, Icahn originality) already covers most of this; this list keeps us
honest and catches drift.

> Core principle: **"inauthentic" = mass production, templates, no human
> fingerprint.** It is NOT a ban on AI. Every episode needs ≥1 identity layer
> that is impossible to mass-produce.

## The 7 checks

1. **Distinct identity (swap test).** Cover the channel name — could our last 5
   thumbnails be swapped with a competitor's and nobody notices? Our moat:
   recurring **mascot character** + **cloned voice**. Keep at least one such
   layer obvious in every upload. ✅ we have two.

2. **Voice.** Never ship a common stock ElevenLabs voice. We use a **cloned
   Cartesia voice** (unique) — keep it. If ever using a guest/second voice, clone
   or hire a real VO, don't grab a stock preset. ✅

3. **Thumbnail fingerprint.** Our house style (3–5 word ALL-CAPS yellow on dark)
   is a template — the exact thing Gemini flags. **Deliberately vary composition
   across consecutive episodes**: subject type (mascot vs real photo vs
   illustration), text placement, color accent within brand. Never ship 5 in a
   row with identical layout. ⚠️ audit each upload against the previous 4.

4. **Visual variety inside the video.** Slideshow / single-image slow pan-zoom is
   the #1 trigger. Keep the mix high: real footage + animated mascot
   (full/aside/boil) + sketch illustrations + radio/waveform + diagrams. Watch
   the **static-photo-Ken-Burns share** — if it's most of the runtime, add motion
   / real footage. Target: photo-only scenes < ~55% of runtime. ⚠️

5. **Repeating word/structure patterns.** Titles must NOT share an opener word or
   identical emotional template across the channel. Vary **script structure**,
   not just topic — rotate among several formats; no two consecutive uploads
   share hook→body→close shape. (CLAUDE.md "5 rotating formats" — actually do it.)
   Don't let vanilla ChatGPT phrasing patterns in. ⚠️ structure is our weak spot.

6. **Net information gain (gist filter).** Before building: Google the topic,
   watch the top 5, answer in one sentence — *what's in ours that's not in any of
   those 5?* If you can't, don't build it. Our Icahn method proves demand; this
   check proves originality. ✅ enforce per episode.

7. **AI disclosure.** Non-disclosure of AI = *deceptive practices* = **channel
   termination** (worse than demonetization). We use synthetic (cloned) voice +
   AI visuals. **Default: mark "altered/synthetic content" YES on upload.** Our
   cartoons aren't realistic people so it's arguably optional, but under this
   crackdown the safe, honest call is to disclose — it costs nothing in reach.
   ⚠️ **flip the default to disclosed.**

## Appeal insurance (if ever hit)
- Consider a **multi-channel network** for the higher-risk channels (animation /
  heavy-AI) — MCNs escalate appeals to real YouTube reps.
- On a hit: **private** (never delete) the most repetitive videos, refresh
  titles/thumbnails, upload 1–2 genuinely different videos within the 7-day
  window. Appeal video with a face on camera + screen-recorded workflow.
- First appeals usually auto-reject → **public pressure**: post the appeal video,
  tag YouTube support, and (EU) demand they name the *specific* uploads flagged.
