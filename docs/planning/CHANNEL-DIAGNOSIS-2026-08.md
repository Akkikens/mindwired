## The five real causes, ranked

1. **Too many mid-tier swings, not enough giant ones.** Near-daily publishing
   of 2/3-recognition topics = many tiny pools. The two giants exploded. The
   catalog's own data now proves the giant-names-on-waves rule (encoded in
   icahn-validate). ~26 videos in a month is production-heavy — repointed at
   giant names on a weekly rhythm, the same effort wins bigger pools.
2. **Tiny seed audience.** ~1.1K subs (mindwired) means every upload
   cold-starts from a small test pool. Fixes: the Shorts trailer funnel
   (8 Shorts for Earhart/Tunguska are RENDERED and undripped in
   out/shorts_final/), monthly same-size collabs (YouTube's official
   Collaborations feature), Communities rhythm, and the breakout videos'
   funnels (21 Astronauts' MORE FROM block was just fixed — it was 5 bare
   URLs on the channel's biggest traffic source).
3. **Packaging style is a generation behind.** Live titles use ALL-CAPS
   clickbait grammar ("INSIDE $150 Billion SPACE STATION Before It's
   DESTROYED", "520 DEAD The DEADLIEST...") — 2025-26 data says 30-50-char
   negative-emotion STATEMENTS win (+20-60% median views) and the ALL-CAPS
   stacked style reads content-farm. The US1549 title test is proving this
   LIVE: "The Real Radio Call Behind the MIRACLE on the Hudson" is winning
   at 43.9% watch-time share. New spec is in ctr-engine (House Style 2.0).
4. **No nameable signature yet.** The channel's differentiator (real
   ATC/docket audio — the Evidence Engine) isn't branded on packaging. The
   US1549 test winner shows "The Real Radio Call..." framing IS the
   signature. Serialize it. (One-time branding call for Akshay.)
5. **The feedback loop only started closing today.** First lessons banked in
   LAUNCH-LESSONS.md 2026-08-03. Every upload before that taught nothing
   systematic.
