## ⚠ DATA CORRECTIONS (baked in — do NOT revert)

1. **The controller did NOT hear "We have some planes" live.** This is the single
   easiest thing to get wrong and nearly every retelling gets it wrong. Staff Statement
   17, verbatim: *"The controller only heard something unintelligible; he did not hear
   the specific words '[w]e have some planes.'"* He knew it was a hijacking only after
   the **next** transmission ("Nobody move…"). Because he had not understood the first
   one, **the Manager of Boston Center instructed the Center's Quality Assurance
   Specialist to "pull the tape," listen to it closely, and report back.** The phrase
   that names this episode was recovered from a *recording*, not heard in the moment.
   The script's cold open is built on exactly this and must not dramatise it into a
   controller gasping at a live transmission. **And the tape was not pulled by the
   controller at his scope** — it was a different person (the Center's quality assurance
   specialist, unnamed in the public report), in a different room, minutes later, and the
   words did not reach the chain of command until roughly **9:03**. The controller,
   **Peter Zalewski** 🧍, says he asked for it and credits others with doing it:
   *"And thankfully, they did pull the tapes."* (NBC News, 9 Sept 2006.)
2. **Never say NORAD "lied."** The Commission found the public accounts **incorrect**,
   and explicitly did not find that the operational people did anything wrong. Any
   stronger characterisation is somebody's opinion and must be attributed to that
   person by name (see §5, pending the accountability research).
3. **Do not say the military "stood down."** The record shows the opposite: it shows
   improvisation against bad information. Staff Statement 17: *"We do not believe that
   an accurate understanding of the events of that morning reflects discredit on the
   operational personnel from NEADS or FAA facilities."* This episode is the antidote to
   the stand-down myth, not a vehicle for it.
4. **The Langley scramble was NOT a response to American 77 or United 93.** It was a
   response to a **phantom** — a mistaken report that American 11 was still airborne and
   heading for Washington. The Commission could not identify the source of that mistaken
   FAA information, and says so. Do not invent a source for it.
5. **"Nine minutes" is the maximum, not the norm.** NEADS got 9 minutes' notice on
   American 11 — *"the most the military would receive that morning of any of the four
   hijackings."* For American 77 it never received a hijack notification at all; for
   United 93 it learned only after the crash.
6. **Times are local (Eastern Daylight Time)** and, per the Commission's own note, are
   rounded to the nearest minute unless a second is given. Keep that convention.
7. **The tapes in this episode are NEADS/NORAD audio. They are NOT cockpit voice
   recordings.** Staff Statement 17 states plainly: *"None of the audio excerpts you will
   hear this morning are derived from cockpit voice recorders."* No CVR audio from any
   9/11 flight has ever been publicly released. Never imply otherwise — and per the
   Evidence Engine honesty rule, real tape audio is labeled **"ACTUAL NORAD/NEADS
   RECORDING"** and anything voiced is labeled **"RECREATION."**
8. **The machine transcripts in `_evidence/noradtapes/transcripts/` are whisper output
   on 8 kHz radio audio and are NOT a citable record.** Use them only to FIND a moment
   on the tape; every quoted line must then be verified against Staff Statement 17, the
   Commission report, or reputable published transcription. Whisper has already been
   observed mangling names on this material (it rendered "Nasypany" as "Sipney").

## ACT 1 — THE SYSTEM THAT MORNING (why none of it worked)

- **[CONFIRMED]** The last hijacking involving US controllers, FAA management and
  military coordination before 9/11 had occurred in **1993** — eight years earlier.
  (SS17, "The FAA and NORAD")
- **[CONFIRMED]** FAA structure: **22 Air Route Traffic Control Centers**; the four
  hijacked aircraft were monitored mainly by **Boston, New York, Cleveland and
  Indianapolis** Centers, plus the **Command Center at Herndon, Virginia**. *"What Boston
  Center knew was not necessarily known by the Centers in New York, Cleveland, or
  Indianapolis."* (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The hijackers **turned off the transponders on three of the four**
  aircraft. Controllers normally do not display primary radar returns; on 9/11 they
  reconfigured their scopes to hunt for them. (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED]** NORAD's alert posture: **two fighters on alert** at each of a small
  number of sites — relevant here, **Otis ANGB** (Cape Cod, Massachusetts) and **Langley
  AFB** (Virginia). NEADS reported to the Continental Region HQ in Florida, which
  reported to NORAD HQ in Colorado Springs. (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED]** The hijack protocol assumed the **pilot** would announce it — by radio
  or by squawking **7500** — and that the military's role would be a **discreet escort**,
  *"vectored to a position five miles directly behind the hijacked aircraft."*
  **The protocols did not contemplate an intercept.** (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED — the thesis of Act 1]** The protocol presumed three things, all false
  that morning: that the aircraft would be identifiable and would not try to disappear;
  that there would be time to work up the chain of command; and that the hijacking would
  *"take the traditional form, not a suicide hijacking designed to convert the aircraft
  into a guided missile."* SS17's own summary: *"the existing protocol was unsuited in
  every respect for what was about to happen."*

## ACT 2 — AMERICAN 11 AND THE TAPE (the cold open)

- **[CONFIRMED]** **8:00** — AA11 begins its takeoff roll at Logan; a 767 for Los
  Angeles, **81 passengers, 11 crew, 24,000 gallons of fuel**. **8:13** — the controller
  instructs *"turn twenty degrees right"*; the flight acknowledges. **That is the last
  transmission it ever answers.** Sixteen seconds later a climb instruction gets nothing.
  (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED]** **8:21** — the transponder goes off. The controller tells his
  supervisor something is seriously wrong; **neither of them suspects a hijacking**, and
  the supervisor tells him to work it as a "no radio" aircraft. (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED — verbatim, the title moment]** **8:24:38**, from American 11:
  *"We have some planes. Just stay quiet, and you'll be O.K. We are returning to the
  airport."* Then, seconds later: *"Nobody move. Everything will be O.K. If you try to
  make any moves, you'll endanger yourself and the airplane. Just stay quiet."* And at
  **8:34**: *"Nobody move please. We are going back to the airport. Don't try to make any
  stupid moves."* (SS17) — **see correction #1: the first one was not understood live.**
- **[CONFIRMED]** Because he had not understood it, **the Manager of Boston Center had
  the Quality Assurance Specialist "pull the tape."** (SS17) This is the episode's
  founding act: on a morning nobody understood, the first thing anyone did that produced
  the truth was **play back a recording**.
- **[CONFIRMED]** **8:25–8:32** — Boston Center notifies its chain of command; **8:28**
  it tells Herndon it believes AA11 is hijacked and heading for New York Center's
  airspace; **8:32** Herndon passes it to FAA headquarters. (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED]** **8:34** — Boston Center, off-protocol, goes at the military directly
  via the FAA's Cape Cod facility, and also tries **a former alert site at Atlantic City,
  unaware it had been phased out.** (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED — verbatim, the birth of the air defense]** **8:37:52**, Boston Center
  reaches NEADS. *"Hi. Boston Center TMU, we have a problem here. We have a hijacked
  aircraft headed towards New York, and we need you guys to, we need someone to scramble
  some F-16s or something up there, help us out."* — **NEADS: "Is this real-world or
  exercise?"** — *"No, this is not an exercise, not a test."* SS17: *"This was the first
  notification received by the military—at any level—that American 11 had been hijacked…
  The air defense of America began with this call."*
- **[CONFIRMED]** Otis F-15s to battle stations immediately; Col. Robert Marr 🧍 phones
  Maj. Gen. Larry Arnold, who tells him *"to go ahead and scramble the airplanes, and
  we'd get permission later."* **Scramble ordered 8:46**; **airborne 8:53**. (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED — verbatim]** NEADS had nowhere to send them: *"I don't know where I'm
  scrambling these guys to. I need a direction, a destination."* (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED]** **8:46:40** — AA11 hits the North Tower. Shortly after **8:50**, while
  NEADS is still hunting for it on radar, word reaches them that a plane has hit the WTC.
  The Otis fighters, lacking a target, are sent to military airspace off Long Island and
  hold there **9:08–9:13**. (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED]** **Nine minutes** of notice — and SS17 notes it was the most they got
  all morning.

## ACT 3 — THE PHANTOM (why the jets went the wrong way)

- **[CONFIRMED]** **9:03** — NEADS learns of United 175 in a call from New York Center
  *"at about the time the plane was hitting the South Tower."* (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED — verbatim]** **9:08**, the NEADS Mission Crew Commander decides not to
  keep the fighters parked offshore: *"We need to talk to FAA. We need to tell 'em if
  this stuff is gonna keep on going, we need to take those fighters, put 'em over
  Manhattan… At least we got some kind of play."* A combat air patrol is established over
  New York at **9:25**. (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED]** **9:09** — Langley fighters to battle stations, as **backup for
  refuelling**, not in response to any new hijacking: *"NORAD had no indication that any
  other plane had been hijacked."* (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED — the phantom, verbatim exchange]** NEADS is told American 11 is still
  airborne and heading for Washington: *"NEADS: Okay. So American 11 isn't the hijack at
  all then, right? / FAA: No, he is a hijack. / NEADS: He—American 11 is a hijack? / FAA:
  Yes. / NEADS: And he's heading into Washington? / FAA: Yes. This could be a third
  aircraft."* SS17: *"We have been unable to identify the source of this mistaken FAA
  information."* (See correction #4.)
- **[CONFIRMED — verbatim]** The MCC to the Battle Commander: *"Okay, uh, American
  Airlines is still airborne. Eleven, the first guy, he's heading towards Washington.
  Okay? I think we need to scramble Langley right now. And I'm gonna take the fighters
  from Otis, try to chase this guy down if I can find him."* Order issued **9:23**:
  *"Okay … scramble Langley. Head them towards the Washington area."* Transmitted 9:24;
  **airborne 9:30**. (SS17)
- **[CONFIRMED]** **9:34** — NEADS learns American 77 is missing **by chance**, in a call
  it placed itself, at the suggestion of Boston Center's military liaison: a Washington
  Center manager says *"We're looking—we also lost American 77."* SS17 is blunt: *"If
  NEADS had not placed that call, the NEADS air defenders would have received no
  information whatsoever that American 77 was even missing… No one at FAA Command Center
  or headquarters ever asked for military assistance with American 77."*
- **[CONFIRMED — verbatim, the act's payoff]** **9:36**, Boston Center: *"Latest report.
  Aircraft VFR six miles southeast of the White House. … Six, southwest. Six, southwest
  of the White House, deviating away."* The MCC takes control of the airspace — *"Okay,
  we're going to turn it … crank it up. … Run them to the White House"* — and then
  discovers **the Langley fighters are not north toward Baltimore as he intended, but
  east over the ocean**: *"I don't care how many windows you break. Damn it… Okay. Push
  them back."* (SS17)

## ACT 5 — CONFLICTING ACCOUNTS (the reason this episode exists)

- **[CONFIRMED — verbatim, and this is the spine of Act 5]** SS17's own section, titled
  **"Conflicting Accounts"**:
  - NORAD officials testified in **May 2003** that NEADS received hijack notification of
    **United 93 at 9:16**. *"This statement was incorrect. There was no hijack to report
    at 9:16. United 93 was proceeding normally at that time."*
  - They testified that NEADS was notified of the hijacking of **American 77 at 9:24**.
    *"This statement was also incorrect."* The 9:24 notice was that **American 11** had
    not hit the WTC and was heading for Washington — and the NEADS log entry at 9:24
    records *"American Airlines #N334AA hijacked,"* **the tail number of American 11.**
  - They stated the Langley scramble answered American 77 and/or United 93. *"These
    statements were incorrect as well."*
  - *"Yet this response to a phantom aircraft, American 11, is not recounted in a single
    public timeline or statement issued by FAA or DOD… This inaccurate account created
    the appearance that the Langley scramble was a logical response to an actual hijacked
    aircraft."*
- **[CONFIRMED]** What the correction costs the official story, in SS17's words: *"the
  military did not have 14 minutes to respond to American 77, as testimony last year
  suggested. It had at most one or two minutes… and the fighters were in the wrong place
  to be able to help. **They had been responding to a report about an aircraft that did
  not exist.**"* And on UA93: *"Nor did the military have 47 minutes to respond… By the
  time the military learned about the flight, it had crashed."*
- **[CONFIRMED — the fairness paragraph, non-negotiable, must be IN the film]** *"We do
  not believe that an accurate understanding of the events of that morning reflects
  discredit on the operational personnel from NEADS or FAA facilities. The NEADS
  commanders and floor officers were proactive in seeking information, and made the best
  judgments they could based on the information they received."* SS17 also credits FAA
  controllers and managers for thinking *"outside the box"* — the nationwide ground stop
  and the decision to land every aircraft, *"executing that unprecedented order
  flawlessly."*
- **[CONFIRMED]** SS17's own reading of the damage the bad accounts did: *"it was
  inaccurate accounts of what happened that created questions about supposed delays in
  the military's interception of the hijacked aircraft."* — i.e. **the false timeline is
  what fed the conspiracy theories.** That is the episode's closing argument.

## ⭐ THE HINGE — one misread tail number

**[CONFIRMED, [CR-1] + [MONO-9]]** The NEADS Mission Crew Commander's handwritten log
(MCC/T Log) records at **9:24: "American Airlines #N334AA hijacked."**
**N334AA is the tail number of American 11** — the aircraft that had hit the North Tower
38 minutes earlier. Someone reading that log later took it for American 77 and built a
public timeline on it. The 9:16 "UA93" entry is the same error: the log records tail
**N612UA**, which is **United 175**.

details on the NEADS log led him to mistakenly believe"* 9:24 was AA77. [IG]

**[CONFIRMED as an authentic document obtained under the Commission's subpoena, [MONO-9]]**

over the ocean, **Col. Robert Marr** e-mailed ret. Col. Scott:

hijacked at 0916L… **It is easiest to explain the simultaneous scramble order with the

Both halves were false: UA93 was not hijacked until 9:28, and AA77 was never reported

## WHAT ACTUALLY CHANGED (the "so what" act)

- **[CONFIRMED — DOT IG CC-2006-085]** The **Domestic Events Network (DEN)** — a nationwide,
  continuously open telephone line between the agencies with jurisdiction over US airspace.
  This is the direct fix for the 9/11 failure mode: there was no standing open line, so
  every notification meant finding and dialling a number — which is why Boston Center ended
  up phoning a **decommissioned alert site in Atlantic City.**
- **[CONFIRMED]** New controller procedures for passing hijack/suspicious-aircraft
  information over the DEN, and **recording equipment installed on most FAA Washington
  Operations Center lines** — *"Prior to September 11, FAA did not record any WOCC
  telephone lines."*
- **[CONFIRMED]** **Operation Noble Eagle**, launched **12 September 2001** and still
  running. Scale of the shift: **only 14 interceptors were on alert in the continental US
  on 9/11**; NORAD scrambled **7 times** in the 10 Sept–10 Oct window of 2000, and **41
  times** in the same window of 2001.
- **[CONFIRMED]** **SCATANA → ESCAT.** The Cold War plan that contemplated shutting down
  navigation aids nationwide was replaced by the Emergency Security Control of Air Traffic
  plan — final rule **October 2006**, effective **January 2007**, FAA Advisory Circular
  **99-1D**. Regional shutdowns and rerouting replaced the blunt nationwide ground stop.
- **[CONFIRMED in substance, UNVERIFIED in detail]** Shootdown authority was pre-delegated
  below the President after 9/11, with the Secretary of Defense as the accountable
  authority. **The exact ROE and delegation chain are classified — do not name a rank or an
  officer who "can order a shootdown today."**
