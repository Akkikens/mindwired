## DESCRIPTION (paste as one unit)

Eighty-one point seven seconds after Columbia left the pad, a piece of insulating foam came off the external tank and hit the left wing. Nobody knew how big it was, because the tracking cameras that should have recorded it were out of focus or badly sited.

Over the next five days, three separate people asked for a photograph of that wing. The United States military began preparing to take it. The request was cancelled.

This is not a film about foam. It is about the photograph that was requested three times and never taken, and about what the Columbia Accident Investigation Board found when it went looking for the reason — which turned out to have less to do with physics than with how an organisation talks itself past a warning.

Built on the primary record: 32 exhibits from the CAIB report on screen with page citations, Rodney Rocha's own emails, and the verbatim Mission Control transcript from the morning of 1 February 2003.
