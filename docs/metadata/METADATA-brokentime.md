### Description
Your head ages faster than your feet. That's not a thought experiment — NIST
measured it with atomic clocks 33 centimeters apart. And that's the smallest
example of a much bigger problem: no two clocks in the universe agree.

In this video: the GPS satellites built deliberately "wrong" so they become
right in orbit, the Earth's core that's measurably younger than its surface,
the astronaut twins where one came back from space milliseconds younger than
the one who stayed on Earth, why NASA is building an official clock for the
Moon, and whether Interstellar's "1 hour = 7 years" planet could really exist.
