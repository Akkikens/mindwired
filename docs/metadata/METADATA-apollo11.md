## Description

July 20, 1969. As Neil Armstrong squeezed out of the lunar module, a piece of the spacecraft snapped off — silently, in vacuum. It was the switch that armed the ascent engine. The one that would take them home.

This is the complete story of Apollo 11: the Cold War panic that started it, the fire that nearly ended it, the 1202 alarms and 30 seconds of fuel over a boulder field, the felt-tip pen that saved the crew — and then, the strangest chapter: why millions of people decided it never happened at all. We take every major hoax claim seriously, and dismantle each one — the flag, the stars, the shadows, the cameras — ending with the argument nobody can answer: the Soviets watched, and confirmed it.
