## Description (English ONLY)
On September 11, 2001, two hijacked airliners brought down the Twin Towers.
Seven hours later, a third building fell — 7 World Trade Center, 47 stories,
never struck by a plane. It's still the only known case of a modern steel
high-rise collapsing from fire alone. This is the real story of the six-year
federal investigation that figured out why — one steel column, a girder that
quietly lost its seat, and a finding the government itself first called
"physically impossible," then had to walk back on the record.

This documentary is built entirely on NIST's own published findings — the
Federal Building and Fire Safety Investigation of the World Trade Center
Disaster — real exhibits, real quotes, real FAQ citations. We also address,
directly and once, the specific claims people still raise about explosives,
molten metal, and a widely misquoted phrase — each one stated plainly, then
checked against the actual documented record.

Every finding on screen traces to NIST's own reports or FAQ. Every disputed
claim is attributed to whoever made it, never asserted as fact. No depiction
of the human toll beyond what the investigation itself required — this is an
engineering story, not a retelling of the attack.

If this left you with questions about that day, we've also covered United 93
— the fourth hijacked flight — in a separate investigation. Subscribe to
Black Box Breakdown for a new deep-dive every week.
