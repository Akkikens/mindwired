On July 17, 1996, twelve minutes after taking off from JFK, TWA Flight 800 exploded over the Atlantic and killed all 230 people aboard. Dozens of witnesses swore they saw a streak of light rise up to meet the plane. The government spent four years and forty million dollars proving them wrong — and thirty years later, federal FOIA lawsuits are still forcing new documents into the open. This is the full evidence breakdown: the real NTSB verdict, the missile theory that wouldn't die, and the FBI records that just surfaced this year.

Every claim in this video traces to the public record: NTSB Aircraft Accident Report AAR-00/03, NTSB Safety Recommendation Letter A-98-34/-39, the U.S. Navy Salvage Report, and the actual FOIA-released FBI records published by Judicial Watch. The missile theory and the 2022 family lawsuit are reported as the claims of named plaintiffs and advocates, not as established fact; the NTSB's official cause has never been overturned. The cockpit voice recorder exchange is a clearly labeled CVR RECREATION — no public CVR audio exists for this flight under U.S. law.
