## Description (English ONLY — Black Box is not a Hindi channel)
On January 15, 2009, US Airways Flight 1549 lost both engines two minutes after
leaving LaGuardia. What happened next has been told a hundred times — Captain
Chesley Sullenberger's calm voice, the ditching in the Hudson, all 155 people
walking away. This isn't that version.

This is built around the actual radio recordings from that afternoon: the real
mayday call, the three runways offered and declined, the exact moment the crew
committed to the river — all real audio, not a recreation. And it opens somewhere
almost no other documentary on this crash does: inside New York TRACON, with the
air traffic controller who spent thirty minutes believing he'd just listened to a
plane full of strangers die, before anyone told him they'd survived.

Also featured: real US Coast Guard harbor-camera footage of the aircraft floating
in the river, the ferry rescue, and the salvage operation days later — plus what
the NTSB's investigation actually found, why nobody ever sued the airline, and
where the crew is today.

Everything here is grounded in the NTSB's official accident report (NTSB/AAR-10/03),
congressional testimony, and public-domain FAA and US Coast Guard recordings and
footage. The only two dramatized lines are cockpit exchanges whose audio was never
publicly released — clearly labeled as recreations, never passed off as real.
