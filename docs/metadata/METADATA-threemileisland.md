## Description

In Pennsylvania, there is a nuclear reactor that partially melted down in 1979.
This year, it's scheduled to switch back on — not to power homes, but to power
artificial intelligence.

The federal investigation into the 1979 accident found something specific: not
a broken part, but a mindset — an industry so focused on the safety of its
equipment that it downplayed the one thing actually running the plant, the
people in the room. That finding is a real, public federal document. Right now,
a regulator is deciding whether the exact conditions it warned about have
actually been fixed, before Three Mile Island Unit 1 — renamed the Crane Clean
Energy Center — restarts under a 20-year deal to power Microsoft's AI data
centers.

This episode holds the actual 1979 Kemeny Commission report up against the live
2026 restart paperwork, beat by beat.
