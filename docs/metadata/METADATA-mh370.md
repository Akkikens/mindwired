## Description (English only)
On the 8th of March, 2014, a Boeing 777 with 239 people on board took off from Kuala Lumpur, bound for Beijing. Thirty-nine minutes later, it vanished from every radar on Earth. No mayday. No wreckage on the water. No bodies. It has never been found.

This is the complete forensic breakdown of MH370 — the biggest unsolved mystery in the history of aviation, and a case that is still open right now, in 2026.

How does a modern airliner simply disappear? This documentary walks the entire timeline, grounded only in what the evidence actually proves: the calm final words — "Good night, Malaysian three seven zero" — the transponder switching off at the perfect blind spot between two countries, the deliberate turn back across Malaysia, and the seven hourly "handshakes" with a single Inmarsat satellite that became the only witness to where the plane went. We break down how investigators turned a communications satellite into a tracking device, why the math pointed south into the emptiest ocean on Earth, and what the barnacle-covered flaperon that washed up on Réunion Island in 2015 finally proved.

Then we weigh every serious theory — catastrophic fire, decompression and a "ghost flight," hijacking, and the deliberate-diversion theory investigators keep circling back to, including the captain's home flight simulator — honestly, with the case for and against each, and the official verdict on all of them. And we separate that hard evidence from the conspiracy theories (Diego Garcia, "shot down," cover-ups) that the real debris and satellite data quietly kill.

Finally, the search itself: the largest and most expensive in aviation history. Australia's 120,000 km² effort. Ocean Infinity's "no-find, no-fee" gamble — a private company hunting the seabed on its own dime for a $70 million reward. Their latest search ended in January 2026 with nothing, after more than 140,000 km². And in 2026, Malaysia extended the hunt out to 2027. As you watch this, robotic submarines are still out there in the dark, looking.

Everything here is grounded in the official Malaysian Safety Investigation Report (2018), ATSB search data, and Inmarsat's satellite analysis. There is no real cockpit voice recorder — none was ever recovered — so no cockpit dialogue is invented. The only radio transmissions recreated are the known air-traffic-control exchanges, and they are clearly labelled "ATC — RECREATION." No living person is accused of a crime the evidence has never proven.
