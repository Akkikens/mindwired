## Description (English only)
On the morning of September 11th, 2001, United Airlines Flight 93 took off 42
minutes late from Newark. That delay meant something the hijackers never
planned for: by the time four men took over the cockpit, two other hijacked
planes had already struck the World Trade Center — and over 37 phone calls from
the cabin, the passengers and crew found out. At least five of those calls
described something the other hijacked flights never had. Intent to fight back.

This is the full, sourced story of United 93 — built entirely from the 9/11
Commission Report, the declassified (and still never publicly released as audio)

received the passengers' final calls. We show you the actual Commission Report
pages on screen. We also break down a separate, real story the military got

morning didn't match its own tapes — a documented institutional failure, not a
cover-up of the attacks themselves. And we address, directly and with evidence,
the persistent claim that Flight 93 was shot down — because the physical evidence
says otherwise.
