## Description
There is no single "now." Not on Earth, not on the Moon, not anywhere. Every
clock in the universe — from the one thirty-three centimeters above your head
to the ones orbiting a black hole four million times the mass of our Sun —
is running at its own private speed, and physics says that's permanent.

A cinematic tour through gravitational time dilation: the atomic clocks that
proved your head ages faster than your feet, the GPS satellites built
deliberately "wrong" so they become right in orbit, the astronaut who came
home measurably younger than his own twin, and the star that swung past a
supermassive black hole fast enough to prove Einstein right a century after
he wrote the equation down.
