## Description

Spaceflight has killed 21 people. It came within seconds of killing 32 more —
and every single one of them made it home. These are the twelve missions that
should have been disasters: the true stories of every astronaut who almost
died in space, told in order, from 1962 to 2018.

John Glenn reentering with a heat shield warning and flaming debris past his
window. Alexei Leonov locked out of his own spacecraft on the first spacewalk.
Neil Armstrong spinning at 296 degrees per second, seconds from blackout.
Apollo 12 struck by lightning twice — and the parachute secret Mission Control
kept for ten days. Apollo 13's four cold days in a two-man lifeboat. The 21.3 g
ballistic fall of Soyuz 18a. The only pad abort in history, commanded by radio
while the abort wires burned. STS-27 Atlantis — the crew who saw 707 damage
sites and were told it was "lights and shadows." The Mir fire that blocked the
lifeboat, and the collision that gave the crew 23 minutes of air. Luca
Parmitano drowning inside his own helmet. And the bent pin that nearly killed
the crew of Soyuz MS-10 at 50 kilometres.

Every event in this documentary is real and verified against NASA mission
reports, official investigations, crew oral histories and transcripts. No
dramatization is presented as real footage.
