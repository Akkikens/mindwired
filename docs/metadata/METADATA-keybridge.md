## Description

A container ship the length of three football fields passed under the Francis
Scott Key Bridge on March 23rd, 2024. Three days later, it destroyed it.

For two years, the story was simple: a ship lost power, a bridge fell down,
six construction workers died. It was ruled an accident.

Then, on May 12th, 2026, federal prosecutors unsealed an 18-count indictment —
alleging the ship's operators had secretly modified its fuel system as a
cost-cutting measure years earlier, and that the modification is exactly why
the ship couldn't recover power in time.

This is the real minute-by-minute reconstruction, built from the NTSB's actual
259-page final report and the real federal indictment — the loose wire that
started it, the seven-second phone call that saved dozens of lives, the 48
seconds that never reached the road crew, and the criminal case that's still
unfolding right now, with a trial set for 2027.
