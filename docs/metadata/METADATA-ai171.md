## Description (English ONLY)
On June 12, 2025, Air India Flight 171 crashed 32 seconds after taking off from
Ahmedabad, killing 260 people — the deadliest crash in Air India's own history,
and the first fatal hull loss of a Boeing 787 anywhere since the aircraft
entered service in 2011. One passenger, seated 11A, survived.

India's Aircraft Accident Investigation Bureau found something almost
impossible to explain: seconds after liftoff, the fuel-control switches for
both engines moved from RUN to CUTOFF, one second apart. The cockpit voice
recorder captured one pilot asking the other why he cut off the fuel — and the
other saying he didn't. That is where the confirmed facts stop. The full final
report — the one meant to state an actual cause — isn't expected until October
2026, more than a year after the crash. The captain's own 91-year-old father
has publicly disputed how investigators have characterized his son, and two
Indian pilots' associations have called any "pilot suicide" narrative
"reckless and unfounded."

This video draws a hard line between what the AAIB has actually confirmed and
what remains genuinely, officially unresolved — including the competing
electrical-fault theory raised by the Federation of Indian Pilots, which is
also not a proven finding. We cover the flight, the timeline the investigators
established minute by minute, the survivor's own words in his own interviews,
the compensation and litigation that followed, and exactly what is still
missing before this story has a real ending.

A note on fairness: no official body has concluded this crash was caused by
deliberate pilot action. This video does not assert or imply that it was. Where
family members or pilot associations have disputed a reading of the evidence,
their objections are included. When the final report is published, we will
cover it.
