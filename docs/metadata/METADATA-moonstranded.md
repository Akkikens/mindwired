## Description (SEO long-form)
Two days before Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed on the Moon, the White
House had already written the speech announcing their deaths. This is the
complete, fact-checked story of Apollo 11's secret contingency plan — told
through the real archival photographs and the actual declassified memo.

In this full documentary: William Safire's "In Event of Moon Disaster" memo
and the protocol behind it (the calls to the "widows-to-be", NASA deliberately
cutting the radio, the burial-at-sea rite); why the lunar module's single
ascent engine had no backup and no rescue option; Michael Collins' "secret
terror" of coming home alone; the 1202 alarms and the 25 seconds of fuel; the
insurance covers the crew signed because no company would insure them; Apollo
8's 50/50 odds and Apollo 10's near-fatal tumble; the ENGINE ARM circuit
breaker that snapped off inside the cabin — and the felt-tip pen Buzz Aldrin
used to arm the engine that brought them home.

Every event is documented historical fact: the Safire memo (National Archives,
1999), NASA mission transcripts, and the astronauts' own memoirs. No
dramatization — only authentic archival photographs, public domain and
Creative Commons.
