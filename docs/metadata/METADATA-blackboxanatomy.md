## DESCRIPTION

Every plane that has ever crashed left one survivor — and it's never a person. It's the flight recorder: a fist-sized stack of memory wrapped in armor, built to be crushed at 3,400 g, burned at 1,100°C, and sunk for years, then still tell investigators exactly what happened down to the last second.

So what's actually inside a "black box"? Why is it painted bright orange? Who built the first one — and why did the world ignore him for years? In this episode the recorder tells its own story: the man who lost his father to a crash with no answers, the certified survival gauntlet every recorder must pass, the 37.5 kHz pinger that raced a dying battery under the Atlantic, the two-year recovery of Air France 447, and the myths we all believe — graded true, false, or complicated.
