# HOOK-CHECKLIST — the first 15 seconds (and the exits)

Enforced automatically as `preflight_doc.py` warnings; hook_lab
(`scripts/hook_lab.py`, docs/guides/HOOK-LAB.md) generates + scores variants.
Retention logic: the first 30s is the algorithm's hardest-monitored window;
chapter boundaries and the outro are where sessions die.

## Cold open (scenes 1-2)
- [ ] First line = the single most shocking TRUE fact. No greeting, no
      "in this video", no logo (wordmark comes AFTER the teaser).
- [ ] First scene ≤ 30 words; strongest visual of the episode under it.
- [ ] A concrete number (stat chip) or card within the first 4 scenes.
- [ ] Punch it with sound: a `stat_hit`/`chapter_boom` cue lands with the fact.
- [ ] Open a loop the title promised — and don't close it before mid-video.
- [ ] 2-3 `hookVariants` written and scored via hook_lab before committing VO.

## Mid-video (chapters)
- [ ] Every chapter name is a curiosity gap, and every chapter ENDS on a
      mini-cliffhanger line that names what's next.
- [ ] Scene visual changes at least every 2 narration lines; no 3-scene cycling.

## Exit (final scenes)
- [ ] Verbal bridge naming a SPECIFIC next video with a curiosity tease
      ("If X shocked you, wait until you see Y…") — spoken, not just a card.
- [ ] Standard subscribe line, then the channel's baked outro.
