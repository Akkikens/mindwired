📌 KEY FACTS:
• The Treaty of Versailles (1919) placed full war guilt, crushing reparations and harsh military limits on Germany.
• Hyperinflation (1923) and the Great Depression (1929) destroyed both the economy and hope.
• In that despair, Hitler and the Nazi Party rose to power (Chancellor, January 1933).
• Appeasement (the Munich Agreement, 1938) failed to stop him — it emboldened him.
• After the Nazi–Soviet pact (August 1939), Germany invaded Poland on 1 September 1939 — and the war began.
• With Pearl Harbor (December 1941), it became a truly global war.
