## One honest note on reach (not part of the upload checklist, but relevant to what you said)

Good SEO/tags help discovery, but they aren't the main lever for a channel not
getting traction yet — the two that actually move the needle at this stage are:
1. **Thumbnail + title CTR** (the packaging, not the tags) — see `THUMBNAILS.md`.
2. **Upload frequency/consistency** — YouTube's algorithm needs a pattern of
   uploads to learn who to show your channel to; sporadic uploads reset that.
The subscribe outro we just built addresses the third lever (converting
viewers who *do* find you into subscribers) — tags/hashtags are the smallest
of the four levers. If reach stays flat after a few more uploads with strong
thumbnails, that's the next thing worth diagnosing, not the metadata.
