## Description (English ONLY — Black Box is not a Hindi channel)
On the 29th of October 2018, a brand-new Boeing 737 MAX fought its own pilots for
thirteen minutes and dived into the Java Sea. Five months later, a second
identical jet did almost exactly the same thing over Ethiopia. 346 people died —
killed by the same aircraft, in the same way, twice.

This is the full story of how it happened: not a freak accident, but a chain of
business decisions. To beat the Airbus A320neo, Boeing bolted bigger engines
onto a 1960s airframe and promised airlines the plane would fly exactly like the
old 737 — so pilots would need no simulator training. To keep that promise, they
added a hidden piece of software called MCAS, let it fire off a single angle-of-
attack sensor with no backup, quietly made it four times more powerful than the
version regulators reviewed, and deleted it from the pilots' manuals. Most 737
MAX pilots had never heard its name.

We break down MCAS with original animated diagrams, walk through both crashes
minute by minute — Lion Air 610 and Ethiopian 302 — and follow the reckoning:
the 20-month grounding, the ~$20 billion cost, the congressional hearings and the
internal messages ("designed by clowns … supervised by monkeys"), the $2.5
billion settlement, and the fact that not one Boeing executive went to prison.

Everything in this documentary is grounded in official findings — the U.S. House
Transportation Committee's final report, the Indonesian (KNKT) and Ethiopian
accident reports, the DOT Inspector General, and the U.S. Department of Justice.
No dramatization, no AI imagery for real events — only authentic public-domain
and Creative Commons archival photographs, plus original diagrams. Note: Boeing
technical pilot Mark Forkner, the only individual ever charged, was acquitted.
