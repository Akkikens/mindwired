## ⚠ SHAREALIKE EXPOSURE — decision owed
`hooks_club_crop`, `nhm_beak`, `nhm_headlimbs`, `tepapa_specimen*` are **CC BY-SA**.
As discrete full-frame stills in a montage this is arguably a collection, not an
adaptation. **Burning them into graded/composited sequences invites the argument that
the whole video must be released under ShareAlike.** Keep them as clean full-frame
beats; prefer the CC0/PD substitutes (`squid_size_comparison_b.png`, `architeuthis.jpg`)
wherever the shot allows.

## Derived segments (cut from the logged sources above — same licences apply)
Every file below is an ffmpeg trim of a clip already logged in this file. No new
rights, no new sources. Cut so the film uses **distinct moments** of real footage
rather than repeating one shot.

- `co_*.mp4` — **the entire cold open (h1-h16)**, cut from `ifremer_abyss`,
  `ifremer_abyssfish`, `ifremer_rovarm` (Ifremer, CC BY 4.0) and `noaa_glasssquid`
  (NOAA Ocean Exploration, public domain). Rebuilt 2026-09-01 after the relevance
  audit found the stock fetch had put a laptop screensaver, glass marbles, a
  Dubrovnik timelapse and "Pivot Irrigation in Saudi Arabia" under the first 60
  seconds. CLAUDE.md's hardest retention rule is that the first 30-60s must be real
  motion footage; it now is, end to end.

  logged sources.
