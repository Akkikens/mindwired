# Free clip credits — endoftime documentary (freeclips2 / supplementary pool)

Second batch of free/legal cinematic space b-roll, mined to reduce repetition
across the 34-min documentary. Sources: **ESO** (CC BY 4.0), **ESA/Hubble**
(CC BY 4.0), and **Pexels** (Pexels License — free commercial use, no
attribution legally required, but credited below as courtesy). All files
verified with ffprobe: H.264 MP4, ≥1920px wide, 5–60 s. Clips longer than 60 s
at download were trimmed to an interior segment (audio stripped) to stay in
spec; 4K sources trimmed to 1920×1080.
