All hand-cut from **`StarfishPrimeInterimReportByCommanderJTF8`**
(archive.org, PD Mark 1.0) — https://archive.org/details/StarfishPrimeInterimReportByCommanderJTF8
— the real declassified U.S. government film covering the July 9, 1962
Starfish Prime shot, including the "Fishbowl Auroral Sequences" reel filmed
from Samoa, Mauna Loa (Hawaii), and Tonga, and the "Fishbowl XR Summary"
compilation of all 5 rocket-launched Fishbowl tests.

  real auroral-bloom footage, "Fishbowl Auroral Sequences" segment,
