# hadalpollution — video provenance

Every clip below is **NOAA Ocean Exploration**, a US federal work in the public
domain. NOAA requests the credit line "NOAA Ocean Exploration" — it is in the
video description.

**These are real deep-sea ROV dives, but they are NOT hadal.** NOAA works mostly
at 300-3,000 m and has no amphipod, snailfish, hadal, Mariana or abyssal footage
at all; genuine hadal film belongs to Schmidt/MBARI/Caladan, which are NC or
permission-only and stay barred. No caption in this episode asserts a depth or a
location the footage cannot support. The two animals the script actually names
are carried by real STILLS of those exact species, credited in images/ATTRIBUTION.md.
