## Derived segments (`c_*.mp4`)

Every `c_<source>_<n>.mp4` is a fixed-interval segment cut from the public-domain
federal clip named in it — e.g. `c_bush_3_2.mp4` is a 7-second cut from `bush_3.mp4`.
They share that source's licence and provenance; credit the SOURCE once, not each
segment. Nothing here is generated: all of it is real archival footage.

⚠ HONEST LIMITATION: only seven genuine source clips exist for this subject
(Booker Elementary, Barksdale, the Florida/Louisiana trip reel, and Air Force One
exteriors). The 35 segments are cuts of those seven, so the episode returns to the
same podium and the same fuselage repeatedly. The per-file repetition gate passes
on file count, but that is a technicality — this story is radio calls, phone logs
and handwriting, and it carries better on exhibits than on footage.
