# ATTRIBUTION — spacexlunarimpact (mindwired)

All public domain (US federal government works, 17 U.S.C. §105). Verified real
and downloaded 2026-08-04, before any scripting, per Akshay's explicit priority
("real footage first 30 secs is prio").

- `ranger7_impact.m4v`, `ranger8_impact.m4v`, `ranger9_impact.m4v` — real
  transmitted camera footage from NASA's Ranger 7/8/9 probes (1964-65),
  running to the moment each deliberately crashed into the Moon. 320×240 (the
  actual era-accurate TV-transmission resolution, not a fetch artifact).
  Confirmed by frame inspection: real crescent-Moon approach shots and the
  iconic rapid-zoom camera-mosaic sequence seconds before impact. Source:
  NASA/JPL history media CDN (d2pn8kiwq2w21t.cloudfront.net) — public domain.
  **The strongest real-footage match for this episode's actual subject: real
  footage of a spacecraft hitting the Moon.**
- `blueghost1_launch_isolated.mp4` — NASA's isolated launch-camera views of the
  Jan 15, 2025 Falcon 9 launch carrying Firefly's **Blue Ghost Mission 1**
  (landed on the Moon Mar 2, 2025) — the mission whose spent upper stage is
  the subject of this episode. NOTE: earlier validation notes in this repo
  mis-recorded this as "Mission 2" — corrected 2026-08-04 (see memory
  `icahn-spacex-lunar-impact`); that same launch also carried ispace's
  Hakuto-R Mission 2 as a co-passenger, likely source of the mix-up. 4K,
  18:53. Source: images.nasa.gov (KSC-20250115-MH-AJN01-0001), credited
  NASA/SpaceX (NASA's own edited cut of launch-camera views, distinct from
  SpaceX's separately CC BY-NC-licensed livestream branding) — public domain.
- `lcross_impact.mp4` — NASA TV broadcast of the 2009 LCROSS mission's
  deliberate Centaur-stage impact into Cabeus crater. 6:04. Source:
  archive.org mirror of NASA TV (nasa_tv-LCROSS_Lunar_Impact) — public domain.
- `lro_moontour_notitle.mp4` — NASA Goddard SVS "Tour of the Moon" (item
  4619), built from real LRO camera (LROC) + laser altimeter (LOLA) data.
  Generic lunar-surface flyover — no Einstein-Crater-specific flyover exists
  in NASA's SVS/LROC catalogs (checked directly). 4:40, 1080p. Public domain.

## Deliberately not used as raw footage
NASA's Apollo 13 booster-impact SVS visualization (svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4803) is
a **data reconstruction**, not camera footage — no camera ever filmed an S-IVB
impact at the moment it happened (there was no camera on the booster and none
on the Moon aimed at the site). If used, it must be labeled on screen as a
visualization, same honesty rule as NIST's collapse simulations on Black Box.

## What does NOT exist yet
Real footage/imagery of the actual Aug 5, 2026 impact and its plume. Confirmed
via live search 2026-08-04: every source is future-tense; the impact window is
~06:35 UTC Aug 5, 2026 and has not occurred as of this fetch. LRO's before/after
comparison pass is scheduled ~Aug 12, 2026. **Re-check after both dates before
scripting or showing any observational claim about the event itself.**
