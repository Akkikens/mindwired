## ⛔ WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY CUT
NASA's own description of JSC-1592A ends: "and Apollo 1 capsule - post fire (CBS pool
footage)." **Pool footage shot by CBS belongs to CBS, even inside a NASA reel.** Three
slices from 51 minutes onward showed the charred capsule and were deleted rather than
shipped. The aftermath in this episode comes from JSC-1592B, which is NASA-shot.

Same trap elsewhere in this subject, all avoided: the C-SPAN3 rebroadcasts of 1967 CBS
coverage on archive.org; the KSC-1969 Apollo 8/12 compilation, which opens with a CBS
Special Report; Periscope Film's watermarked transfer of the 1966 NASA newsreel; and any
clip from *First Man* (2018), which is Universal's and which dominates YouTube search for
this subject.
