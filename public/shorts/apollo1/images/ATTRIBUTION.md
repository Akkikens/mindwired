### Deleted as wrong-subject (do not re-add)
- `block1_hatch` via keyword search returned *"a closeup view of a block found by Apollo 16 crewmen"* — a rock. Replaced with the real AS-204 hatch above.
- `crew_portrait_2.jpg` was S68-53187, the **Apollo 8** prime crew — it would have appeared under Apollo 1 narration because `img` resolves by prefix.
- `pad34_saturn1b_1.jpg` was S68-48788, an **Apollo 7** launch. Apollo 1 never flew; launch footage cannot appear in this episode.
- `mims_florida_rail`, `apollo1_patch`, `borman_testimony_1967`, `white_room_pad34`, `board_report_cover`, `baron_report_page` — no correct asset was found; those scenes were repointed to sourced material or to exhibits.

## Exhibits — scanned pages of the primary record

All rendered with `pdftoppm` at 300 dpi from the PDFs in
`public/shorts/_evidence/apollo1/docs/`. Both documents are US federal government
works in the public domain.

**Source A — *Report of Apollo 204 Review Board to the Administrator, NASA*
(NASA-TM-84105), 5 April 1967** → `apollo204_review_board_summary.pdf`

- `ex_board_cover_1.png` — cover page (page 1)
- `ex_transmittal_1.png` — the Board's transmittal letter to Administrator James E. Webb, dated 5 April 1967, signed by every member incl. Frank Borman (page 3)
- `ex_gmt_note_1.png` — the report's own "CONVERSION OF TIME" note: times are GMT, subtract 17 hours for EST (page 2)
- `ex_rb_test_purpose_1.png` — the purpose and designation of the Plugs Out Integrated Test (OCP FO-K-0021-1)
- `ex_rb_hazard_rule_1.png` — the rule under which the test was not classified hazardous
- `ex_rb_finding2_1.png` — Finding 2: combustible material adjacent to ignition sources; 16.7 psia, 100% oxygen; "The test conditions were extremely hazardous."
- `ex_rb_finding4_1.png` — Finding 4: the inner hatch could not be opened prior to rupture
- `ex_rb_finding5_1.png` — Finding 5: those responsible failed to identify the test as hazardous; no emergency procedures
- `ex_rb_finding9_1.png` — Finding 9
- `ex_finding1_1.png` — page 6-1, Findings 1–5, carrying **"No single ignition source of the fire was conclusively identified"** and **"No evidence was discovered that suggested sabotage."**
- `ex_rb_transmission_first_1.png` — analysis of the first transmission, beginning 23:31:04.7 GMT
- `ex_rb_transmission_second_1.png` — analysis of the clear portion, "Got a fire in the cockpit"
- `ex_rb_transmission_three_readings_1.png` — **"The entire second transmission is garbled and is, therefore, subject to wide variation of interpretation … and no definitive transcription is possible,"** followed by the three interpretations the Board printed
- `ex_cable_destroyed_1.png` — condition of the cabling recovered from the module
- `ex_board_mundane_1.png` — the Board's own account of what it could and could not establish

**Source B — *Apollo 204 Accident*, Report of the Committee on Aeronautical and
Space Sciences, US Senate (Senate Report No. 956, 90th Congress), 30 Jan 1968** →
`senate_report_956.pdf`

- `ex_senate_overconfidence_1.png` — the committee on overconfidence in the programme
- `ex_senate_hatch_balance_1.png` — the committee on hatch egress time (at least 90 seconds)
- `ex_senate_phillips_finding_1.png` — page 7, "NASA-Spacecraft Contractor Relations": the Phillips task force review, and the testimony that its findings "had no effect on the accident, and were not related to the accident"
- `ex_senate_no_single_person_1.png` — **"No single person bears all of the responsibility…"**
- `ex_senate_hope_1.png` — the committee's *hope* that the remainder of the programme would be carried out with greater understanding and dedication

### Removed
- `ex_phillips_letter_1.png` — was **byte-identical** to `ex_senate_phillips_finding_1.png`, and scene a6_3 displayed it while quoting Phillips' "I am definitely not satisfied with the progress and outlook of either program." That line is **not on that page** and is not in `CLAIMS-apollo1.md`. The scene was rewritten to quote only text that is actually on the record in hand.
