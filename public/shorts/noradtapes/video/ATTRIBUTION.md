## Honesty note baked into the edit
None of this footage is from 11 September 2001, and none of it depicts NEADS on that day.
No camera recorded the NEADS operations floor that morning; the tape is what survives.
These clips stand in for the *kind* of room and the *kind* of launch, never for the event
itself, and no caption claims otherwise. The same applies to the Pentagon aerials, which
are modern photographs of the building, not images of the 2001 damage.
