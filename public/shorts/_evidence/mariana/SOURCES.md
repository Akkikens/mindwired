HONEST LABELING: the Challenger Deep clip is real hydrophone audio FROM the
trench. The whale/earthquake clips are real NOAA hydrophone recordings but from
OTHER arrays — label "NOAA HYDROPHONE" (real), representing the CLASS of sound
the 2016 Challenger Deep recording captured (Dziak et al. 2017, Oceanography);
never claim they are the specific Challenger Deep whale/quake.

NOT USED (rights): Cameron/Deepsea Challenger (NatGeo), Vescovo/Limiting Factor
(Discovery/Caladan), Nereus imagery (WHOI request-required), Jamieson snailfish
footage (Minderoo/UWA — not blanket PD). Facts narrated; illustrated with NOAA PD.
