## ⛔ TRAPS — established, not theoretical
1. **The noradtapes archive.org collection sits in the same folder as copyrighted network
   broadcast dubs.** Provenance must be established per FILE. A collection being
   predominantly PD proves nothing about any individual item in it.
2. **FEMA 403 photography carries individual photographer copyrights that do NOT inherit
   the report's PD mark.** The wtccollapse episode used only the diagrams, deliberately.
   Same rule applies here.
3. **No network broadcast footage, ever** — CNN/NBC/ABC/CBS/Fox/local. Content-ID
   fingerprinted regardless of clip length; there is no short-clip safe harbour, and a
   strike risks the channel's monetization rather than one video. This is the constraint
   that shapes the entire episode: every large competitor in this lane wins on exactly
   the material we cannot touch.

4. **⛔ NEW, 2026-09-14 — the "NIST FOIA" corpus on Wikimedia is NOT safely public domain.**
   Wikimedia blanket-tags files titled "NIST FOIA" as PD. At least one file in that corpus
   is titled with a **WPIX credit** — a commercial news station. The PD tag is being applied
   to the FOIA *release*, not to the underlying footage's authorship, which is exactly the
   noradtapes archive.org trap one layer further up the supply chain. **Recommendation:
   exclude the entire corpus** rather than adjudicate it file by file; the downside of one
   misjudgment is the channel's monetization.
5. **⛔ NEW — C-SPAN is not blanket public domain.** Only House and Senate FLOOR proceedings
   are. Committee hearings — including the 9/11 Commission hearings — carry C-SPAN's own
   licence. Do not source hearing video from C-SPAN.
6. **⛔ NEW — a DVIDS item can be PD-hosted and still not be archival.** "As It Happened —
   The 9/11 Pentagon Attack" (DVIDS 506581, 2017) is a modern audio-over-RECREATION, not
   2001 footage. PD status answers "may we use it"; it does not answer "is it real." Both
   questions must be asked of every asset, and anything reconstructed must be labelled.
7. **NIST NCSTAR photo appendices are an UNSCREENED risk of the FEMA 403 pattern** —
   third-party photographer credits inside a federal report. Screen before use. (The EPA
   OIG report was checked and is clean.)
