## Description (SEO long-form — English first, then Hindi)
60 to 80 million people died in just six years. This is the whole story of the
Second World War — from the first shot in Poland to the last surrender aboard
the USS Missouri — told through real archival photographs.

World War II — The Whole Story is a complete, fact-checked documentary covering
the entire war: the Treaty of Versailles and the rise of Hitler, Blitzkrieg and
the fall of France, Dunkirk, the Battle of Britain, Operation Barbarossa,
Pearl Harbor, the Holocaust, the war in the desert, Stalingrad, Midway, D-Day,
the fall of Berlin, Hiroshima and Nagasaki — and the story most documentaries
skip: the 2.5 million Indian soldiers who formed the largest volunteer army in
history, from the Burma campaign and Kohima to the Bengal famine.

Every event in this video is documented historical fact. Sources include the
Imperial War Museums, the National WWII Museum, and public archives. No
dramatization — only authentic archival photographs, public domain and
Creative Commons (full attribution below).

Subscribe to mindwired for a new complete documentary every week.
