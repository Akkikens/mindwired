## Description (English first, then Hindi)
Nothing in the first photograph of this documentary looks dangerous — a watch
factory in Orange, New Jersey, 1922. But the women who worked there were
swallowing radium every day, and behind every photo that follows, somebody died.

Every Way the Atom Has Killed a Human is a complete, fact-checked,
feature-length documentary about every fatal radiation disaster in history —
told through the real archival photographs that survived. The Radium Girls.
The Demon Core that killed two Los Alamos physicists nine months apart. The
Kyshtym explosion the Soviet Union denied for 30 years. The Windscale fire.
SL-1 — the only fatal reactor accident in American history. The submarine
K-19. The blue flash of criticality accidents. Chernobyl — the night, the
firefighters, the liquidators, and the truth about the "suicide divers". The
glowing powder of Goiânia. Tokaimura's 83 days. The lost radioactive sources
of Ciudad Juárez and Kramatorsk. Fukushima — and why the fear killed more
people than the radiation. And the near-misses: Damascus, Goldsboro, and the
submarine officer who refused to fire.

Every event in this video is documented historical fact. Sources include the
IAEA accident reports, the NRC, WHO and UNSCEAR assessments, and declassified
Soviet-era records. No dramatization, no AI imagery for real events — only
authentic archival photographs, public domain and Creative Commons.
