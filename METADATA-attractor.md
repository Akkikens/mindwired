## Description

Right now, our entire galaxy is being pulled across the universe at over two million kilometers an hour — toward something we still cannot see. They call it the Great Attractor.

In this episode we trace one of the strangest mysteries in cosmology: why the Milky Way is defying the expansion of the universe and falling toward a single hidden point. From the afterglow of the Big Bang that revealed our motion, to the Zone of Avoidance that hid the culprit behind our own galaxy, to the gravitational basin of Laniakea, the Dipole Repeller pushing us from behind, and the even greater Shapley Concentration beyond — we follow the invisible currents all the way to an unsettling truth about where we're going, and whether we'll ever arrive.
